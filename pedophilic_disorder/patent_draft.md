## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Pedophilic Disorder by co-administering (a) a
serotonin reuptake inhibitor that is also a 5-HT1A receptor partial
agonist and (b) a non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist, in
particular the combination of vilazodone and bicalutamide. The
invention is intended only for adult subjects with a current
diagnosis of Pedophilic Disorder per the *Diagnostic and
Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text
Revision* (DSM-5-TR), in whom pedophilic urges or fantasies are
accompanied by clinically significant distress or impairment, or by
clinically meaningful risk of acting on minors, and who voluntarily
receive treatment within an active psychiatric or forensic
management setting. The invention is not a substitute for legal
supervision, victim-protection measures, mandatory reporting, or
child-safety restrictions where otherwise required.

## Background of the invention

Pedophilic Disorder is defined in DSM-5-TR as recurrent, intense
sexually arousing fantasies, sexual urges, or behaviors involving
sexual activity with a prepubescent child or children, generally
age 13 years or younger, that have persisted for at least six
months, where the individual has either acted on these sexual
urges or the urges / fantasies cause marked distress or
interpersonal difficulty. The diagnosis is limited to individuals
who are at least 16 years of age and at least 5 years older than
the child or children in the fantasies or behavior. The
corresponding ICD-11 construct similarly requires persistence of
the arousal pattern together with distress, impairment, or risk to
others.

The prevalence of Pedophilic Disorder in the general population is
uncertain and likely under-reported. The clinical burden is
substantial, not only because of distress in a subset of subjects
but more importantly because of the grave risk to minors if the
condition is acted upon. The disorder is associated with high rates
of depression, anxiety, obsessive–compulsive symptoms, substance
use disorders, and recurrent legal / forensic involvement.

The current standard of care is non-pharmacological and forensic in
the first instance. It consists primarily of structured
psychotherapy, relapse-prevention planning, external monitoring,
and where appropriate court- or system-mandated supervision. No
pharmaceutical product is approved by the United States Food and
Drug Administration (FDA) for the treatment of Pedophilic Disorder
as a primary indication. Off-label pharmacotherapy in this domain
has historically relied on (i) selective serotonin reuptake
inhibitors (SSRIs) for intrusive ideation, compulsive sexual
behavior, and distress, and (ii) anti-androgen or GnRH-agonist
regimens to reduce libido in the most severe high-risk cases. The
published evidence base is limited and ethically difficult to
standardize.

The mechanistic gap that the present invention addresses has two
components. First, pedophilic ideation and preoccupation may have a
compulsive and intrusive-cognitive component with mechanistic
overlap with obsessive–compulsive and addictive phenotypes.
Vilazodone (Viibryd®) is an orally available, FDA-approved
antidepressant that combines selective serotonin reuptake
inhibition with partial agonism at the 5-HT1A receptor. The dual
mechanism is reported to support attenuation of anxious-compulsive
symptomatology and intrusive ideation. The serotonergic axis
therefore addresses the *intrusive-ideation and compulsive-
preoccupation* substrate of Pedophilic Disorder, but does not
directly attenuate androgen-receptor-mediated sexual drive.

Second, the androgen-mediated component of pedophilic sexual urge
intensity is mediated by androgen receptor signaling in the central
and peripheral nervous systems. Hormonal anti-androgen agents
substantially reduce circulating testosterone and can reduce
paraphilic urge intensity, but their adverse-effect burden
(decreased bone mineral density, hot flashes, gynecomastia, mood
disturbance, hepatic effects, cardiovascular effects, and complete
loss of consensual sexual function) has limited their acceptability
and tolerability. The non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist
bicalutamide (Casodex®) is FDA-approved for prostate carcinoma and
competes with endogenous testosterone and dihydrotestosterone at
the androgen receptor *without* lowering circulating gonadotropin
or testosterone. Bicalutamide therefore offers a *partial* and
*titratable* attenuation of androgen-receptor-mediated drive
without the total-hypogonadism profile of GnRH agonist therapy.

No issued patent or published application known to the present
inventors discloses the use of a 5-HT1A-partial-agonist SSRI in
combination with a non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist for
Pedophilic Disorder specifically.

The present invention recognizes that serotonergic attenuation of
intrusive preoccupation (a vilazodone HTR1A axis) and non-
castrating titratable reduction of androgen-receptor-mediated urge
(a bicalutamide AR axis) are complementary and convergent
substrates for treatment of Pedophilic Disorder in adults with
clinically significant distress or clinically meaningful risk of
acting on minors. The combination is expected to produce a greater
reduction in pedophilic-distress and pedophilic-urge measures than
either agent alone at any individually achievable dose, with a
substantially better tolerability profile than legacy hormonal
anti-androgen regimens. The invention is intended only as an
adjunct to rigorous child-safety, legal, and forensic management.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) vilazodone or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof, and (b) bicalutamide or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Pedophilic Disorder in an adult subject in need thereof,
comprising co-administering to the subject a therapeutically
effective amount of vilazodone and a therapeutically effective
amount of bicalutamide. Co-administration may be simultaneous, in
a unitary dosage form or in separate dosage forms, or sequential,
with each agent administered within a window of about 24 hours of
the other.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) vilazodone or a salt thereof in a first unitary dosage form,
(b) bicalutamide or a salt thereof in a second unitary dosage
form, and (c) written instructions for the treatment of
Pedophilic Disorder in an adult subject according to a dosing
regimen disclosed herein, together with a written informed-consent
and shared-decision-making module, a forensic risk-management
worksheet, and explicit child-safety warnings.

The invention contemplates vilazodone in a daily dose of about
10 mg to about 40 mg administered orally with food once daily,
with a preferred adult dose of 20 to 40 mg/day after a 7-day
titration from 10 mg/day. The invention contemplates bicalutamide
in a daily dose of about 25 mg to about 150 mg administered orally
once daily, with a preferred adult dose of 50 mg/day. The mass
ratio of vilazodone to bicalutamide (calculated on the free-base
equivalents) ranges broadly from about 1:15 to about 4:1, and is
preferably from about 1:5 to about 1:1.

The technical effect of the combination is a reduction, in adult
subjects with Pedophilic Disorder, in intrusive pedophilic
preoccupation, pedophilic urge intensity, and clinician-rated risk
of acting on minors, that is greater than the sum of the
individual effects of vilazodone and bicalutamide administered as
monotherapies at any individually achievable dose, while preserving
endogenous gonadotropin and testosterone secretion relative to
GnRH-agonist therapy.

the meanings set forth below.

*"Bicalutamide"* means the compound (R,S)-N-[4-cyano-3-
(trifluoromethyl)phenyl]-3-[(4-fluorophenyl)sulfonyl]-2-hydroxy-2-
methylpropanamide, marketed in the United States as Casodex®.
Bicalutamide is a non-steroidal competitive androgen receptor

includes the racemate, the active R-enantiomer, and any
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, hydrate, polymorph,
prodrug, isotopically labeled form, or metabolically equivalent

*"Pedophilic Disorder"* means the disorder defined in DSM-5-TR:
recurrent, intense sexually arousing fantasies, sexual urges, or
behaviors involving sexual activity with a prepubescent child or
children, generally age 13 years or younger, that have persisted
for at least 6 months, where the person has either acted on these
sexual urges or the urges or fantasies cause marked distress or
interpersonal difficulty. The diagnosis is limited to individuals
who are at least 16 years old and at least 5 years older than the
child or children in the fantasies or behavior.

*"Pedophilic urge"* means an experienced internal pressure toward
the disorder-defining fantasies or behaviors associated with
Pedophilic Disorder.

*"Forensic risk-managed treatment setting"* means an outpatient,
partial-hospital, inpatient, or correctional / forensic psychiatric
setting in which the subject receives active psychiatric treatment,
risk monitoring, and explicit child-safety restrictions.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount of an active
agent which, when administered to an adult subject for the
treatment of Pedophilic Disorder according to a dosing regimen
disclosed herein, produces a clinically meaningful improvement in
at least one of: clinician-rated pedophilic-urge severity,
intrusive-preoccupation score, responder rate, risk-of-harm
rating, or investigator-rated CGI-I.

*"Co-administer"* means administer two active agents to a subject
so that the pharmacological effect of one agent is exerted on the
subject during the period in which the pharmacological effect of
the other agent is also exerted, regardless of whether the two
agents are administered in the same dosage form, in separate
dosage forms, at the same time, or at different times within a
window of about 24 hours.

### 2. The two active agents

#### 2.1 Vilazodone

Vilazodone is an orally bioavailable selective serotonin reuptake
inhibitor and 5-HT1A receptor partial agonist. By inhibiting the
serotonin transporter, vilazodone elevates synaptic serotonin
across multiple central serotonergic targets; by partially
agonizing the 5-HT1A receptor, vilazodone is reported to support a
more rapid attenuation of anxious-compulsive symptomatology than a
pure SSRI. Vilazodone is FDA-approved for the treatment of major
depressive disorder in adults at oral doses of 10 mg/day
(initial), titrated to 20 mg/day after one week and to 40 mg/day
thereafter, taken once daily with food to support oral
bioavailability. Vilazodone is metabolized primarily by CYP3A4 and
is a substrate (and weak inhibitor) of CYP2C19 and CYP2D6.
Vilazodone is generic in the United States.

#### 2.2 Bicalutamide

Bicalutamide is a non-steroidal competitive antagonist at the
androgen receptor with no agonist activity. By competitively
displacing testosterone and dihydrotestosterone from the AR
ligand-binding domain, bicalutamide attenuates AR-mediated
transcriptional activity in central and peripheral target tissues
*without suppressing endogenous gonadotropin or testosterone
secretion*. Bicalutamide is FDA-approved for the treatment of
advanced prostate carcinoma at oral doses of 50 mg/day.
Bicalutamide is metabolized primarily by CYP3A4. Bicalutamide is
generic in the United States. The present invention adopts the
50 mg/day dosing as the starting dose for adult subjects with
Pedophilic Disorder, with optional titration up to 100 mg/day in
non-responders.

### 4. Dosage forms

Vilazodone is preferably formulated as a once-daily immediate-
release oral tablet at strengths of 10 mg, 20 mg, and 40 mg
(free-base equivalent), matching the marketed product. The tablet
should be taken with food to support oral bioavailability.

Bicalutamide is preferably formulated as a once-daily immediate-
release oral tablet at strengths of 25 mg, 50 mg, and 100 mg.

A kit form of the invention comprises (a) a 30-tablet bottle of
vilazodone tablets at the appropriate dose-titration strength,
(b) a 30-tablet bottle of bicalutamide 50 mg tablets, and
(c) a printed insert with administration timing, titration
schedule, a written informed-consent and shared-decision-making
module, a forensic risk-management worksheet, and explicit child-
safety warnings.

### 5. Dosing regimens

For adults (subjects ≥ 18 years and ≥ 50 kg) with Pedophilic
Disorder, the preferred dosing regimen is:

* Vilazodone 10 mg orally once daily with food on days 1–7, then
  20 mg once daily with food on days 8–14, then 40 mg once daily
  with food thereafter.
* Bicalutamide 50 mg orally once daily, with no titration. In
  non-responders at 12 weeks, the dose may be increased to
  100 mg/day with monitoring for hepatic adverse effects.

There is no pediatric treatment population for the present
invention.

Hepatic adjustment: vilazodone should be reduced or avoided in
moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment per the prescribing
information. Bicalutamide should be used with caution in moderate
hepatic impairment and avoided in severe hepatic impairment; serum
aminotransferases should be measured at baseline, monthly for the
first 4 months, and periodically thereafter.

Renal adjustment: no specific adjustment is required for either
agent in mild-to-moderate renal impairment; in severe renal
impairment, dose review is recommended.

Medication–medication interactions: avoid co-administration with
strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (clarithromycin, ketoconazole,
ritonavir, itraconazole) without halving the vilazodone dose.
Avoid co-administration with strong CYP3A4 inducers.
Co-administration of bicalutamide with warfarin requires INR
monitoring.

### 6. Subject populations

The methods of the present invention are directed to adult subjects
who meet DSM-5-TR criteria for Pedophilic Disorder and who
voluntarily receive pharmacotherapy within an active psychiatric or
forensic risk-managed setting. Inclusion criteria for the preferred
treatment population include: age ≥ 18 years; current diagnosis of
Pedophilic Disorder per DSM-5-TR for at least 6 months; baseline
clinician-rated pedophilic-urge severity above a clinically
meaningful threshold; intact hepatic function (ALT and AST
≤ 1.5 × upper limit of normal); cognitive capacity to participate
in informed-consent processes; and active psychiatric or forensic
supervision with explicit child-safety restrictions. Exclusion
criteria include known hypersensitivity to either active, current
treatment with a prohibited concomitant medication (see § 5),
active psychosis, current mania, uncontrolled substance use
disorder, severe hepatic disease, and inability to comply with
forensic or child-safety restrictions.

### 7. Mechanistic rationale

Pedophilic Disorder is maintained by (a) intrusive and compulsive
sexual preoccupation, (b) an androgen-receptor-mediated drive
component, and (c) in some subjects, progressive disinhibition or
risk escalation in the absence of adequate containment.

Vilazodone, by combining selective serotonin reuptake inhibition
with 5-HT1A partial agonism, addresses the intrusive-preoccupation
component and associated anxious / compulsive amplification.
Bicalutamide, by competitively antagonizing the androgen receptor
*without castrating* the subject, addresses the drive component in
a titratable manner that preserves endocrine continuity and avoids
the full hypogonadal burden of GnRH-agonist therapy. The two axes
are non-overlapping at the receptor level and converge on the
prefrontal–striatal–hypothalamic circuit supporting pedophilic
preoccupation and urge.

Co-administration is therefore expected to produce greater-than-
additive reduction in pedophilic urge intensity, intrusive
preoccupation, and clinician-rated risk than either monotherapy

(`all_disorders_taxonomy_aware_master_summary.csv`), is 28,
indicating mechanistic compatibility above the reporting threshold
for the indication.

### 8. Pre-clinical support

In-vitro assays useful for confirming the mechanistic basis of the
invention include: SERT binding (³H-citalopram displacement);
5-HT1A binding and functional partial agonism assays for
vilazodone; androgen receptor binding and functional antagonism
assays for bicalutamide; and combined salience-network and
reward-regulation assays where available.

Pre-clinical pedophilic behavior is not directly modelable in
animals, and no animal assay is asserted to reproduce the human
disorder. However, in-vivo paradigms in rodent that probe relevant
underlying substrates include compulsive-behavior paradigms,
impulse-control tasks, and androgen-driven sexual motivation
assays. A supra-additive effect of the combination on compulsive
salience and androgen-driven motivation, relative to either
monotherapy at any individually achievable dose, is the principal
in-vivo proof point that motivates the Phase 2 design in § 9.

### 9. Clinical study designs

#### 9.1 Phase 1 — pharmacokinetics and medication–medication interaction

A single-center, open-label, three-period, fixed-sequence study in
24 healthy adult male volunteers (age 18–45 years). Period 1:
vilazodone titration to 40 mg/day for 7 days; full PK sampling on
days 1 and 7 with steady-state. Period 2 (after 7-day washout):
bicalutamide 50 mg/day for 28 days; full PK sampling on days 1, 14,
and 28. Period 3 (after appropriate washout): combination at the
same doses; PK sampling for both agents on days 1 and 14. The
primary endpoint is the geometric mean ratio for AUC₀–τ and Cmax of
vilazodone and of bicalutamide in combination versus monotherapy,
with 90 % confidence intervals contained within 0.80–1.25.

#### 9.2 Phase 2 — randomized, double-blind, three-arm

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study of
the combination in adult Pedophilic Disorder (FIG. 4). Three arms,
1:1:1 randomization, target N = 60 (20 per arm) given the rarity
and ethical / forensic complexity of the indication.

* Arm A (combination): vilazodone titrated to 40 mg/day +
  bicalutamide 50 mg/day.
* Arm B (active monotherapy): vilazodone titrated to 40 mg/day +
  matching placebo for bicalutamide.
* Arm C (placebo): matching placebo for both, with structured
  psychotherapy and standard forensic management.

All subjects receive structured psychotherapy, relapse-prevention
planning, and active child-safety restrictions. The primary
endpoint is change from baseline in clinician-rated pedophilic-urge
severity at week 16. Secondary endpoints include intrusive-
preoccupation score, responder rate, risk-of-harm rating, system
burden, and CGI-I.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Pedophilic Disorder in adult subjects comprising co-administration
of a therapeutically effective amount of the SSRI / 5-HT1A partial
agonist vilazodone and a therapeutically effective amount of the
non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist bicalutamide are
disclosed. The two agents act on non-overlapping receptor classes
that converge on paraphilic-urge and compulsive-preoccupation
