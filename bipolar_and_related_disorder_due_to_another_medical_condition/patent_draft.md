## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Bipolar and Related Disorder Due to Another
Medical Condition (BRD-AMC) by co-administering (a) a dopamine D2
receptor antagonist and (b) a serotonin 5-HT2A receptor inverse
agonist / antagonist, in particular the combination of perospirone
and pimavanserin. The invention is intended for subjects whose
manic, hypomanic, mixed, or cyclic mood symptoms are attributable
to a defined medical condition and who continue receiving standard
care for that underlying condition; it does not assert disease-
modification of the underlying medical condition itself.

## Background of the invention

Bipolar and Related Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition is
recognized in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 as a syndrome in which elevated,
expansive, or irritable mood, with or without increased energy,
goal-directed activity, decreased need for sleep, disinhibition, or
mixed symptomatology, is the direct physiologic consequence of a
medical condition. The underlying medical condition may include
endocrine disease, autoimmune or inflammatory disease, neurologic
injury, seizure disorder, medication-induced physiologic states,
infectious or metabolic disorders, or other medically recognized
causes of secondary manic-spectrum phenomena.

The prevalence of BRD-AMC is difficult to estimate because the mood
syndrome is often coded under the primary medical diagnosis rather
than as a discrete bipolar-spectrum disorder. Nonetheless, the
clinical burden is substantial. Subjects may experience irritability,
pressured behavior, reduced sleep, impulsive decision-making,
agitation, psychotic features, reduced adherence to treatment of the
underlying medical condition, and major caregiver burden. Unlike
primary bipolar disorder, the course is often shaped by activity of
the underlying medical condition, systemic inflammation, endocrine
flux, medication exposure, or neurologic instability.

The current standard of care is supportive and etiology-directed.
It consists primarily of treating the underlying medical condition,
removing provoking agents where feasible, optimizing sleep, and
using antipsychotic or mood-stabilizing medications empirically
when clinically necessary. No pharmaceutical product is approved by
the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) specifically
for BRD-AMC as a primary indication. Conventional antipsychotic
monotherapy is often used, but it may be limited by motor burden,
sedation, orthostasis, metabolic load, or incomplete control of
salience distortion and mixed-spectrum irritability.

The mechanistic gap that the present invention addresses has two
components. First, medically caused bipolar-spectrum states
frequently involve dopaminergic over-attribution of salience,
goal-directed overactivation, impulsive behavioral output, and in
some cases psychotic admixture. Perospirone is an atypical
antipsychotic approved in Japan for schizophrenia that functions as
a D2 receptor antagonist with additional serotonergic properties.
In the present invention, the D2-antagonist axis addresses the
*manic salience, impulsive overactivation, and psychotic admixture*
substrate of BRD-AMC.

Second, medically driven manic or mixed states may include cortical
5-HT2A-mediated amplification of salience, irritability, and
distorted interpretation of internal or external stimuli. Pimavanserin
(Nuplazid®) is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist
approved by the FDA for hallucinations and delusions associated
with Parkinson disease psychosis. Pimavanserin has no clinically
meaningful D2 antagonism and therefore can modify 5-HT2A-mediated
salience physiology without directly increasing dopaminergic motor
burden. The selective 5-HT2A axis therefore addresses the
*irritability, salience amplification, and psychosis-adjacent
interpretive distortion* substrate of BRD-AMC.

No issued patent or published application known to the present
inventors discloses the use of a D2 antagonist in combination with
a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist for BRD-AMC
specifically.

The present invention recognizes that attenuation of dopaminergic
overactivation (a perospirone D2 axis) and attenuation of
5-HT2A-mediated salience amplification (a pimavanserin axis) are
complementary and convergent substrates for treatment of BRD-AMC.
The combination is expected to produce greater symptomatic
improvement in irritability, reduced need for sleep, pressured
behavior, and psychotic admixture than either agent alone at any
individually achievable dose, without asserting disease-
modification of the underlying medical condition.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) perospirone or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof, and (b) pimavanserin or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Bipolar and Related Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition in a
human subject in need thereof, comprising co-administering to the
subject a therapeutically effective amount of perospirone and a
therapeutically effective amount of pimavanserin. Co-administration
may be simultaneous or sequential, with each agent administered
within a window of about 24 hours of the other.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) perospirone or a salt thereof in a first unitary dosage form,
(b) pimavanserin or a salt thereof in a second unitary dosage form,
and (c) written instructions for the treatment of BRD-AMC
according to a dosing regimen disclosed herein while continuing
standard care for the underlying medical condition.

The invention contemplates perospirone in a daily dose of about
4 mg to about 48 mg administered orally in one or two divided doses,
with a preferred adult dose of 8 mg to 24 mg/day. The invention
contemplates pimavanserin in a daily dose of about 17 mg to about
34 mg administered orally once daily, with a preferred adult dose
of 34 mg/day.

The technical effect of the combination is a reduction, in subjects
with BRD-AMC, in manic / hypomanic symptom severity, irritability,
sleep-loss-driven destabilization, psychotic admixture, and
caregiver burden, that is greater than the sum of the individual
effects of perospirone and pimavanserin administered as
monotherapies at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have
the meanings set forth below.

*"Perospirone"* means the atypical antipsychotic marketed in Japan
for schizophrenia that acts as an antagonist at dopamine D2
receptors, an inverse agonist / antagonist at 5-HT2A receptors, and
a partial agonist at 5-HT1A receptors. The term includes
perospirone free base, salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs,
prodrugs, isotopically labeled forms, and metabolically equivalent
analogues.

*"Pimavanserin"* means the selective serotonin 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist marketed in the United States as Nuplazid®.
The term includes pimavanserin free base, tartrate or other
pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs,
prodrugs, isotopically labeled forms, and pharmacologically
equivalent 5-HT2A inverse agonists.

*"Bipolar and Related Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition"*
or *"BRD-AMC"* means the DSM-5-TR / ICD-11 syndrome in which
elevated, expansive, or irritable mood with increased energy or
related bipolar-spectrum symptoms is the direct physiologic
consequence of a defined medical condition.

*"Manic / mixed-symptom composite"* means a clinically meaningful
burden of elevated or irritable mood, decreased need for sleep,
pressured behavior, psychomotor overactivation, or psychotic
admixture quantified by structured clinician assessment or
validated mania rating instruments.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount of an active
agent which, when administered to a human subject for the
treatment of BRD-AMC according to a dosing regimen disclosed
herein, produces a clinically meaningful improvement in at least
one of: manic / mixed-symptom composite, irritability score,
sleep-loss index, psychotic admixture, caregiver burden, or
investigator-rated CGI-I.

*"Co-administer"* means administer two active agents to a subject
so that the pharmacological effect of one agent is exerted on the
subject during the period in which the pharmacological effect of
the other agent is also exerted, regardless of whether the two
agents are administered in the same dosage form, in separate
dosage forms, at the same time, or at different times within a
window of about 24 hours.

### 2. The active agents

#### 2.1 Perospirone

Perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic developed and approved in
Japan for schizophrenia. It acts as a D2 antagonist, 5-HT2A
inverse agonist / antagonist, and 5-HT1A partial agonist. In the
present invention, the principal mechanistic contribution of
perospirone is reduction of manic salience, impulsive over-
activation, and psychotic admixture through D2 antagonism.
Perospirone is generally used in adult schizophrenia at oral doses
in the range of about 8 mg to 48 mg/day.

#### 2.2 Pimavanserin

Pimavanserin is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist
approved by the FDA for hallucinations and delusions associated
with Parkinson disease psychosis. It has no clinically meaningful
affinity for dopamine D2 receptors. In the present invention, the
principal mechanistic contribution of pimavanserin is modification
of 5-HT2A-mediated salience amplification and irritability-linked
interpretive distortion without direct D2 burden. The labeled adult
dose is 34 mg orally once daily, with no dose titration required.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise perospirone and
pimavanserin, together with at least one pharmaceutically
acceptable excipient. The compositions may take the form of a
co-packaged kit of two separate dosage forms, such as a perospirone
tablet and a pimavanserin capsule or tablet.

Excipients useful for the perospirone composition include diluents
(microcrystalline cellulose, lactose monohydrate), binders
(povidone, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose), disintegrants
(crospovidone), lubricants (magnesium stearate), and coating
polymers. Excipients useful for the pimavanserin composition
include capsule fill excipients, diluents, disintegrants, and
stabilizers suitable for the marketed pimavanserin formulation
profile.

### 4. Dosage forms

Perospirone is preferably formulated as an immediate-release oral
tablet. Pimavanserin is preferably formulated as an oral capsule or
tablet suitable for once-daily dosing.

A kit form of the invention comprises (a) a 30-day supply of
perospirone tablets, (b) a 30-day supply of pimavanserin capsules
or tablets, and (c) written instructions aligned to continuation of
the underlying medical-condition treatment and mood / sleep
monitoring.

### 5. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with BRD-AMC, the preferred dosing regimen is:

* Perospirone 4 mg orally twice daily for 7 days, then 8 mg twice
  daily thereafter, with titration up to 12 mg twice daily if
  clinically needed.
* Pimavanserin 34 mg orally once daily.

Broad genus ranges include perospirone 4–48 mg/day and
pimavanserin 17–34 mg/day. The preferred schedule uses a morning
and evening perospirone regimen and a once-daily pimavanserin
regimen at a consistent clock time.

Hepatic and renal adjustment: severe hepatic or renal impairment is
exclusionary until dedicated dose-finding data are generated in
this indication.

Medication–medication interactions: avoid co-administration with
strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers without review of pimavanserin
exposure. Corrected QT interval monitoring should be considered
when other QT-prolonging medications are used.

### 6. Subject populations

The methods of the present invention are directed to subjects with
BRD-AMC. Inclusion criteria for the preferred treatment population
include: clinician-confirmed bipolar-spectrum mood disturbance
attributable to a defined medical condition; clinically meaningful
manic or mixed symptoms at baseline; stable management of the
underlying medical condition for at least 4 weeks where clinically
possible; and capacity to participate in supportive treatment where
indicated. Exclusion criteria include primary bipolar I or II
disorder without a medical-condition etiology, active delirium
requiring different emergent management, severe hepatic or renal
impairment, and known hypersensitivity to either active.

### 7. Mechanistic rationale

BRD-AMC is characterized by two interacting layers: a
dopaminergically amplified salience / overactivation layer and a
5-HT2A-mediated irritability / psychosis-adjacent salience
distortion layer. Together these can produce sleep-loss spirals,
impulsive action, and severe caregiver burden.

Perospirone addresses the overactivation / behavioral-output layer
through D2 antagonism. Pimavanserin addresses the salience-
amplification and psychosis-adjacent layer through 5-HT2A inverse
agonism without direct D2 burden. The two axes are non-overlapping
at the receptor level and converge on the manic / mixed-symptom
phenotype.

Co-administration is therefore expected to produce greater-than-
additive reduction in irritability, pressured behavior, sleep-loss
destabilization, and psychotic admixture than either monotherapy

indicating mechanistic compatibility above the reporting threshold

### 8. Pre-clinical support

In-vitro assays useful for confirming the mechanistic basis of the
invention include: D2 receptor binding and functional antagonism
assays for perospirone; 5-HT2A binding and inverse-agonism assays
for pimavanserin; and combined salience / arousal network assays
where available.

In-vivo paradigms appropriate for probing the relevant substrates
include hyperactivity and mania-like paradigms, sleep-fragmentation
and circadian destabilization paradigms, psychosis-like salience
tasks, and disease-relevant endocrine or neurologic models that
produce secondary manic phenotypes. A supra-additive effect of the
combination on overactivation, irritability, and sleep-loss
destabilization, relative to either monotherapy at any
individually achievable dose, is the principal in-vivo proof point
that motivates the Phase 2 design in § 9.

### 9. Clinical study designs

#### 9.1 Phase 1 — pharmacokinetics and medication–medication interaction

A single-center, open-label, three-period, fixed-sequence study in
24 healthy adult volunteers (age 18–55 years). Period 1:
perospirone titrated to 16 mg/day for 7 days; Period 2:
pimavanserin 34 mg/day for 7 days; Period 3: the combination for
7 days. Full PK sampling, ECG monitoring, and tolerability
monitoring are obtained in each period. The principal purpose is to
establish tolerability and absence of clinically meaningful PK
interaction.

#### 9.2 Phase 2 — randomized, double-blind, three-arm study

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study of
the combination in adult BRD-AMC (FIG. 4). Three arms, 1:1:1
randomization, target N = 120 (40 per arm).

* Arm A (combination): perospirone + pimavanserin.
* Arm B (active monotherapy): perospirone + placebo for
  pimavanserin.
* Arm C (placebo): placebo for both, with standard-of-care medical
  management.

All subjects continue treatment of the underlying medical
condition. The primary endpoint is change from baseline in manic /
mixed-symptom composite at week 12. Secondary endpoints include
irritability score, sleep-loss index, psychotic admixture,
caregiver burden, and CGI-I.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Bipolar and Related Disorder Due to Another Medical Condition
comprising co-administration of a therapeutically effective amount
of the D2 antagonist perospirone and a therapeutically effective
amount of the 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist pimavanserin are
disclosed. The two agents act on non-overlapping manic-salience and
irritability-amplification circuits implicated in medically caused
manic or mixed mood states and are expected to produce greater-
than-additive symptomatic improvement than either agent alone at
any individually achievable dose while continuing standard care for
the underlying medical condition.
