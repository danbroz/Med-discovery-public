# Fixed-dose pharmaceutical products for treating Fetishistic Disorder with vilazodone and bicalutamide

**U.S. Provisional Patent Application under 35 U.S.C. 111(b)**

## Cross-reference to related applications

None. This specification is prepared as a first-filed U.S. provisional application and does not claim priority to any earlier application.

## Field of the invention

The invention relates to fixed-dose pharmaceutical products, fixed-dose combination product packages, methods of treatment, kits, dose-ratio embodiments, release-profile embodiments, and manufacturing embodiments for treating Fetishistic Disorder with vilazodone and bicalutamide.

## Background of the invention

Fetishistic Disorder is a central-nervous-system, psychiatric, neurocognitive, sleep, substance-use, or mental-health condition with remaining need for better-tolerated, more adherent, and more precisely monitored pharmacologic regimens.

The present disclosure converts a coadministration concept into a fixed-dose medication product posture. The active agents are selected to engage HTR1A agonism + androgen receptor antagonism. The fixed-dose posture is limited by route feasibility, component contribution, compatibility, stability, release performance, safety monitoring, and written-description support.

This specification does not represent completed combination testing. Statements marked MODELED are based on repository metadata, public-source search links, known pharmacologic context, and drafting inference. Statements marked PROPHETIC describe prospective work. Statements marked DATA NEEDED identify information that must be generated or verified before relying on broader claim scope.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a single physical fixed-dose pharmaceutical product comprising fixed amounts of vilazodone and bicalutamide for use in treating Fetishistic Disorder.

The route-feasibility classification for this application is **single_form_fdc**. MODELED: both repository route records resolve to oral; a single physical fixed-dose dosage form is feasible in principle, subject to compatibility, stability, release, and bioavailability data.

In one embodiment, the product is single physical oral fixed-dose tablet or hard capsule. Dose strengths are disclosed as low, standard, and high fixed-dose levels derived from individual-active dose contexts, subject to label review, pharmacokinetic review, tolerability review, and component-contribution confirmation.

In additional aspects, the invention provides methods of treatment, kit instructions, manufacturing routes, stability protocols, dissolution or route-performance protocols, and component-contribution study designs.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

"Fetishistic Disorder" means the disorder as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent clinical diagnostic framework, including only subtypes and severity levels supported by the disclosure.

"Fixed-dose pharmaceutical product" means a product in which predetermined amounts of two active agents are supplied in a single physical dosage form or in route-specific dosage units packaged and labeled for a fixed regimen.

"Single physical dosage form" means a tablet, capsule, film, spray, injection, depot, or other dosage unit in which both active agents are contained in one administered unit.

"Fixed labeled regimen" means written instructions that specify the strength, route, timing, titration, monitoring, contraindication checks, and discontinuation criteria for the packaged active agents.

"MODELED" means a statement derived from computational, repository, mechanistic, public-source, or pharmacologic rationale without completed combination testing.

"PROPHETIC" means a prospective example, study design, manufacturing protocol, or endpoint framework that has not been carried out for the disclosed fixed-dose product.

"DATA NEEDED" means evidence that is not presently represented by this package and must be generated or verified before final filing reliance, clinical use, or non-provisional claim expansion.

### 2. Active agents

Vilazodone is the first active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a 5-HT1A partial agonist and serotonin reuptake inhibitor, with reference route oral, reference dosage form immediate-release tablet with food, dose context 10 to 40 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP3A4 (primary), and safety liability serotonin syndrome with MAOI; GI side effects.

Bicalutamide is the second active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a non-steroidal androgen receptor antagonist, with reference route oral, reference dosage form once-daily tablet, dose context 50 to 150 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP3A4 / glucuronidation, and safety liability hepatotoxicity; gynecomastia.

A pharmaceutically acceptable salt, hydrate, solvate, polymorph, isotopically labeled form, prodrug, or co-crystal is included only for embodiments with written-description, manufacturing, analytical, and clinical-pharmacology support.

### 3. Fixed-dose product strategy

The fixed-dose product strategy is single physical oral fixed-dose tablet or hard capsule.

### 8. Safety, interactions, and monitoring

Administration includes baseline medication review, contraindication screening, adverse-event monitoring, endpoint tracking, and route-specific counseling.

* vilazodone: serotonin syndrome with MAOI; GI side effects
* bicalutamide: hepatotoxicity; gynecomastia
* vilazodone metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4 (primary)
* bicalutamide metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4 / glucuronidation
* serotonergic toxicity and MAOI contraindication screening are required when vilazodone is present
* hepatic monitoring is required or must be justified for actives with hepatotoxicity concerns

For paraphilic-disorder embodiments, subject selection, endpoint tracking, and safety monitoring are limited to lawful and ethically approved clinical treatment or research contexts.

### 9. Subject populations

Preferred subjects have confirmed Fetishistic Disorder, a defined baseline endpoint burden, and inadequate response, intolerance, residual symptoms, relapse risk, adherence limitations, or functional impairment after at least one standard-care intervention, unless a protocol justifies first-line investigational use.

Exclusion criteria include high-acuity psychiatric or medical states requiring immediate intervention, contraindications to either active agent, unsafe interacting medications, pregnancy or lactation concerns where relevant, inability to follow route-specific instructions, and inability to complete the monitoring plan.
