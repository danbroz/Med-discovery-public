## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Acute Stress Disorder (ASD) by co-administering
(a) a serotonin 5-HT2A receptor inverse agonist / antagonist, in
particular pimavanserin, and (b) a melatonergic MT1 / MT2 receptor
agonist with 5-HT2C antagonist activity, in particular
agomelatine. The invention is intended for subjects with clinically
significant acute trauma-related symptoms arising within the acute
post-trauma window and addresses hypervigilance, dissociative /
perceptual stress amplification, intrusive stress reactivity,
sleep disruption, and early functional destabilization. The
invention is not intended for routine prophylaxis after all trauma
exposure, for erasure of autobiographical memory, or for
interference with forensic recall.

## Background of the invention

Acute Stress Disorder is recognized in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 as a
trauma- and stressor-related disorder that develops after exposure
to actual or threatened death, serious injury, or sexual violence
and is characterized by clinically significant symptoms such as
intrusions, negative mood, dissociation, avoidance, and arousal
occurring in the acute period following the trauma. The syndrome
occupies the interval between immediate traumatic shock and longer-
duration post-traumatic syndromes.

The prevalence of ASD is clinically meaningful in emergency,
assault, disaster, accident, military, and acute medical-trauma
populations. The burden includes severe insomnia, panic-like
distress, dissociative episodes, inability to return to ordinary
function, poor treatment adherence after injury, and increased risk
of later chronic post-traumatic psychopathology. In many subjects
the most disabling early features are nighttime hyperarousal,
intrusive trauma-linked perceptual salience, and unstable cognitive
control under acute stress.

The current standard of care is supportive and trauma-informed. It
consists primarily of safety stabilization, sleep support,
psychoeducation, monitoring, and trauma-focused psychotherapy or
other early intervention when clinically indicated. No
pharmaceutical product is approved by the United States Food and
Drug Administration (FDA) specifically for the treatment of ASD as a
primary indication. Borrowed anxiolytic or antidepressant strategies
may address fragments of the syndrome but often do not directly
address the combination of acute salience amplification and severe
sleep / circadian disruption that characterizes early post-trauma
instability.

The mechanistic gap that the present invention addresses has two
components. First, ASD frequently includes cortical threat-salience
amplification, dissociative-perceptual instability, and rigid
assignment of threat meaning to trauma reminders. Pimavanserin
(Nuplazid®) is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist
approved by the FDA for hallucinations and delusions associated
with Parkinson disease psychosis. Pimavanserin has no clinically
meaningful D2 antagonism and therefore can modify 5-HT2A-mediated
salience physiology without direct dopaminergic motor burden. The
pimavanserin axis therefore addresses the *acute threat-salience and
perceptual-stress amplification* substrate of ASD.

Second, ASD is frequently accompanied by severe insomnia, circadian
disorganization, reduced nighttime recovery, and early-morning
reactivity that perpetuate intrusive distress. Agomelatine is a
melatonergic antidepressant that acts as a potent agonist at MT1
and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C receptors. This
combination supports resynchronization of circadian timing,
improvement in sleep architecture, and reduction of nocturnal
hyperarousal. The agomelatine axis therefore addresses the
*circadian, sleep-linked, and recovery-failure* substrate of ASD.

No issued patent or published application known to the present
inventors discloses the use of a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist /
antagonist in combination with a melatonergic MT1 / MT2 agonist
having 5-HT2C antagonism for ASD specifically.

The present invention recognizes that attenuation of acute threat-
salience amplification (a pimavanserin axis) and restoration of
sleep-linked recovery and circadian stability (an agomelatine axis)
are complementary and convergent substrates for treatment of ASD.
The combination is expected to produce greater symptomatic
improvement in acute stress severity, dissociative / perceptual
instability, sleep disruption, and functional re-stabilization than
either agent alone at any individually achievable dose, while
preserving memory integrity and not asserting prevention use in all
trauma-exposed individuals.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) pimavanserin or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof, and (b) agomelatine or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, co-crystal,
or prodrug thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically
acceptable carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Acute Stress Disorder in a human subject in need thereof,
comprising co-administering to the subject a therapeutically
effective amount of pimavanserin and a therapeutically effective
amount of agomelatine. Co-administration may be simultaneous or
sequential, with each agent administered within a window of about
24 hours of the other.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) pimavanserin or a salt thereof in a first unitary dosage form,
(b) agomelatine or a salt thereof in a second unitary dosage form,
and (c) written instructions for the treatment of ASD according to
a dosing regimen disclosed herein in conjunction with trauma-
informed supportive care and sleep-focused stabilization.

The invention contemplates pimavanserin in a daily dose of about
17 mg to about 34 mg administered orally once daily, with a
preferred adult dose of 34 mg/day. The invention contemplates
agomelatine in a daily dose of about 25 mg to about 50 mg
administered orally at bedtime, with a preferred initiation dose of
25 mg nightly and optional escalation to 50 mg nightly after about
2 weeks if clinically indicated. The inventive feature is the
concurrent engagement of 5-HT2A-linked threat-salience modulation
and melatonergic sleep / circadian stabilization in the acute
post-trauma syndrome.

The technical effect of the combination is a reduction, in subjects
with ASD, in acute stress-severity burden, hypervigilance,
dissociative / perceptual instability, and sleep disruption, that is
greater than the sum of the individual effects of pimavanserin and
agomelatine administered as monotherapies at any individually
achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Pimavanserin"* means the selective serotonin 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist marketed in the United States as Nuplazid®.
The term includes pimavanserin free base, pharmaceutically
acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs,
isotopically labeled forms, and pharmacologically equivalent
5-HT2A inverse agonists.

*"Agomelatine"* means the melatonergic antidepressant
N-[2-(7-methoxy-1-naphthyl)ethyl]acetamide or a pharmaceutically
acceptable salt, solvate, hydrate, polymorph, co-crystal, or
prodrug thereof. Agomelatine acts as an agonist at MT1 and MT2
receptors and as an antagonist at 5-HT2C receptors.

*"Acute Stress Disorder"* or *"ASD"* means the trauma- and stressor-
related disorder defined in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 arising in the
acute period after trauma and characterized by clinically
significant symptoms such as intrusions, negative mood,
dissociation, avoidance, and arousal.

*"Acute-stress-severity score"* means a clinically meaningful burden
of intrusion, dissociation, hypervigilance, sleep disruption, or
avoidance quantified by structured clinician assessment or
validated acute-stress instruments.

*"Trauma-informed supportive care"* means care that prioritizes
safety, stabilization, sleep, physiologic recovery, and avoidance
of coercive or retraumatizing practices.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount of an active
agent which, when administered to a human subject for the treatment
of ASD according to a dosing regimen disclosed herein, produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in at least one of: acute-stress-
severity score, sleep-quality score, dissociation score,
hypervigilance score, avoidance burden, or investigator-rated
CGI-I.

*"Co-administer"* means administer two active agents to a subject
so that the pharmacological effect of one agent is exerted on the
subject during the period in which the pharmacological effect of
the other agent is also exerted, regardless of whether the two
agents are administered in the same dosage form, in separate
dosage forms, at the same time, or at different times within a
window of about 24 hours.

### 2. The active agents

#### 2.1 Pimavanserin

Pimavanserin is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist
approved by the FDA for hallucinations and delusions associated
with Parkinson disease psychosis. In the present invention, the
principal mechanistic contribution of pimavanserin is attenuation
of acute trauma-linked salience amplification and dissociative /
perceptual stress instability.

#### 2.2 Agomelatine

Agomelatine is a melatonergic antidepressant that acts as a potent
agonist at MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C
receptors. In the present invention, the principal mechanistic
contribution of agomelatine is restoration of sleep-linked recovery
and circadian stability in the early post-trauma period.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise pimavanserin and
agomelatine, together with at least one pharmaceutically
acceptable excipient. The compositions may take the form of a
co-packaged kit of two separate dosage forms, such as a
pimavanserin capsule or tablet and an agomelatine tablet.

Excipients useful for the pimavanserin composition include capsule
fill excipients, diluents, disintegrants, and stabilizers suitable
for marketed pimavanserin formulation profiles. Excipients useful
for the agomelatine composition include diluents, binders,
disintegrants, lubricants, and coating polymers suitable for oral
tablet manufacture.

### 4. Dosage forms

Pimavanserin is preferably formulated as an oral capsule or tablet
suitable for once-daily use. Agomelatine is preferably formulated
as an oral tablet suitable for bedtime administration.

A kit form of the invention comprises (a) a short-course supply of
pimavanserin capsules or tablets, (b) a short-course supply of
agomelatine tablets, and (c) written instructions aligned to sleep
stabilization, trauma-informed care, and measurement-based acute
follow-up.

### 5. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with ASD, the preferred dosing regimen is:

* Pimavanserin 17 mg orally once daily, titratable to 34 mg once
  daily if clinically indicated and tolerated.
* Agomelatine 25 mg orally at bedtime for about 14 days,
  titratable to 50 mg nightly if clinically indicated.

Broad genus ranges include pimavanserin 17-34 mg/day and
agomelatine 25-50 mg/day. The preferred schedule uses once-daily
pimavanserin and bedtime agomelatine over the acute symptomatic
window.

Medication-medication interactions: corrected QT interval
monitoring should be considered when pimavanserin is combined with
other QT-prolonging agents. Agomelatine should not be used with
strong CYP1A2 inhibitors and requires hepatic monitoring in
appropriate patients. The regimen is not intended to erase memory of
the trauma or to be administered indiscriminately immediately after
all trauma exposure.

### 6. Subject populations

The methods of the present invention are directed to subjects with
ASD. Inclusion criteria for the preferred treatment population
include: age >= 18 years; recent trauma exposure meeting diagnostic
criteria; clinically significant acute stress symptoms; and
willingness to participate in trauma-informed supportive care.
Exclusion criteria include active delirium, untreated primary
psychotic disorder, severe hepatic disease, clinically significant
baseline QT prolongation, and known hypersensitivity to either
active.

### 7. Mechanistic rationale

ASD often includes two interacting layers: acute threat-salience
amplification with dissociative / perceptual destabilization, and
severe sleep / circadian disruption that prevents physiologic
recovery.

Pimavanserin addresses the threat-salience layer through selective
5-HT2A inverse agonism / antagonism. Agomelatine addresses the
sleep-recovery layer through MT1 / MT2 agonism and 5-HT2C
antagonism. The two axes are non-overlapping at the receptor level
and converge on the same acute post-trauma phenotype.

Co-administration is therefore expected to produce greater-than-
additive reduction in ASD burden than either monotherapy alone. The
master receptor-efficacy summary
(`all_disorders_taxonomy_aware_master_summary.csv`), is 31,
indicating mechanistic compatibility above the reporting threshold
for the indication.

### 8. Pre-clinical support

In-vitro assays useful for confirming the mechanistic basis of the
invention include: 5-HT2A binding and inverse-agonism assays for
pimavanserin; MT1 / MT2 receptor binding and signaling assays for
agomelatine; and combined stress-salience and sleep-recovery assays
where available.

In-vivo paradigms appropriate for probing the relevant substrates
include acute stress models, sleep-fragmentation paradigms,
contextual threat-salience tasks, and dissociation-relevant
perceptual-instability models. A supra-additive effect of the
combination on reduction of acute threat reactivity and restoration
of sleep-linked recovery, relative to either monotherapy at any
individually achievable dose, is the principal in-vivo proof point
that motivates the Phase 2 design in § 9.

### 9. Clinical study designs

#### 9.1 Phase 1 — pharmacokinetics and medication-medication interaction

A single-center, open-label, three-period, fixed-sequence study in
24 healthy adult volunteers (age 18-55 years). Period 1:
pimavanserin 34 mg/day for 7 days; Period 2: agomelatine 25 mg
nightly for 7 days; Period 3: the combination for 7 days. Full PK
sampling, ECG monitoring, liver-function monitoring, and
tolerability assessments are obtained in each period. The
principal purpose is to establish tolerability and absence of
clinically meaningful PK interaction.

#### 9.2 Phase 2 — randomized, double-blind, three-arm study

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study of
the combination in adult ASD (FIG. 4). Three arms, 1:1:1
randomization, target N = 150 (50 per arm).

* Arm A (combination): pimavanserin + agomelatine.
* Arm B (active monotherapy): agomelatine + placebo for
  pimavanserin.
* Arm C (placebo): placebo for both, with trauma-informed
  supportive care.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in acute-stress-
severity score at day 28. Secondary endpoints include sleep-quality
score, dissociation score, hypervigilance score, transition-to-PTSD
risk signal, and CGI-I.

* Stress-prophylaxis and PTSD-adjacent patents disclose broader

* Broader acute depression / anxiety mechanism filings do not

The present invention is distinct from each of the foregoing in
(i) the specific receptor classes combined (selective 5-HT2A
inverse agonism / antagonism + MT1 / MT2 agonism + 5-HT2C
antagonism), (ii) the specific actives (pimavanserin +
agomelatine), and (iii) the specific indication (ASD). No prior-art
disclosure suggests the convergent-circuit rationale on which the
present invention is based.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of Acute
Stress Disorder comprising co-administration of a therapeutically
effective amount of pimavanserin and a therapeutically effective
amount of agomelatine are disclosed. The two agents act on non-
overlapping threat-salience and sleep-recovery circuits implicated
in acute post-trauma destabilization and are expected to produce
greater-than-additive improvement in acute stress severity,
hypervigilance, and sleep disruption than either agent alone at any
individually achievable dose while preserving memory integrity.
