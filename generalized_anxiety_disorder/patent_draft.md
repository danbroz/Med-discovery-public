## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) by
co-administering (a) a melatonergic MT1 / MT2 receptor agonist with
5-HT2C antagonist activity and (b) a serotonin reuptake inhibitor with
5-HT1A partial agonist activity, in particular the combination of
agomelatine and vilazodone. The invention is directed to subjects with
clinically meaningful excessive worry, somatic tension, nocturnal
hyperarousal, sleep disruption, and functional impairment, and is not
intended to pathologize ordinary stress, situational worry, or
adaptive vigilance.

## Background of the invention

Generalized Anxiety Disorder is recognized in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 as a
chronic disorder characterized by excessive and difficult-to-control
worry associated with symptoms such as restlessness, tension, impaired
concentration, fatigue, irritability, muscle tension, and disturbed
sleep. Unlike circumscribed anxiety syndromes, GAD often produces
diffuse, pervasive, and persistent apprehension across multiple life
domains.

The disorder carries substantial functional burden. Subjects commonly
experience impaired work performance, reduced daily efficiency,
avoidance of uncertainty, chronic physiologic overactivation, sleep
loss, and secondary depressive symptoms. Affected patients may also
cycle between rumination at night and autonomic tension during the
day, creating a reinforcing loop of poor recovery and amplified worry.

Current standard care is based on psychotherapy and serotonergic
antidepressants. Although many patients benefit from SSRIs, SNRIs, and
cognitive-behavioral therapy, a meaningful subset remains limited by
partial response, poor sleep recovery, next-day hyperarousal, or
persistent rumination despite reduced panic-like symptoms. There
remains a need for a rational combination that addresses both
serotonergic worry circuitry and the circadian / sleep-recovery layer
that sustains chronic anxiety.

The mechanistic gap addressed by the present invention has two layers.
First, vilazodone is an orally available antidepressant that combines
serotonin reuptake inhibition with partial agonism at the 5-HT1A
receptor. This dual mechanism supports attenuation of worry, anxious
distress, and catastrophic expectancy. Second, agomelatine is a
melatonergic antidepressant that acts as a potent agonist at MT1 and
MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C receptors. This pharmacology
supports circadian resynchronization, improvement in sleep continuity,
and reduction of nocturnal hyperarousal.

The inventors recognized that these mechanisms are complementary.
Vilazodone addresses the daytime anxious-ruminative substrate of GAD,
while agomelatine improves sleep-linked recovery and the overnight
reset of arousal systems. Together they are expected to reduce both
subjective worry burden and the physiologic persistence of anxiety
more effectively than either monotherapy at any individually
achievable dose.

Prior-art searching identified anxiety-related disorder references,
agomelatine formulation references, vilazodone formulation references,
and general anxiety-treatment references. Those references do not
teach the specific combination of agomelatine and vilazodone for
Generalized Anxiety Disorder, nor the worry-reduction plus sleep-
recovery framework disclosed herein.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) agomelatine or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, co-crystal, or prodrug thereof and (b) vilazodone
or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Generalized Anxiety Disorder in a human subject in need thereof,
comprising co-administering a therapeutically effective amount of
agomelatine and a therapeutically effective amount of vilazodone. In
preferred embodiments, vilazodone is administered once daily with food
and agomelatine is administered at bedtime.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) agomelatine in a first dosage form, (b) vilazodone in a second
dosage form, and (c) written instructions for treatment of GAD in
conjunction with psychotherapy, sleep-regularity counseling, or other
standard supportive care.

The invention contemplates agomelatine in a daily dose of about
25 mg to about 50 mg administered orally at bedtime. The invention
contemplates vilazodone in a daily dose of about 10 mg to about 40 mg
administered orally once daily with food after titration.

The technical effect of the combination is expected to be greater than
additive improvement in one or more endpoints selected from the group
consisting of generalized-anxiety severity, worry burden, sleep
quality, somatic-tension burden, role function, and Clinical Global
Impression-Improvement (CGI-I), compared with either monotherapy at
any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Generalized Anxiety Disorder"* or *"GAD"* means generalized anxiety
disorder as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent diagnostic
framework, characterized by excessive and difficult-to-control worry
with clinically meaningful distress or functional impairment.

*"Agomelatine"* means the melatonergic antidepressant
N-[2-(7-methoxy-1-naphthyl)ethyl]acetamide and pharmaceutically
acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, co-crystals,
prodrugs, and metabolically equivalent forms that retain clinically
relevant MT1 / MT2 agonism and 5-HT2C antagonism.

*"Vilazodone"* means the serotonin reuptake inhibitor / 5-HT1A partial
agonist and pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates,
polymorphs, prodrugs, and metabolically equivalent analogues that
retain clinically relevant transporter inhibition and 5-HT1A partial
agonism.

*"Worry burden"* means a clinically meaningful burden of excessive
worry, anticipatory rumination, reassurance seeking, or uncertainty
intolerance quantified by structured clinician assessment or validated
anxiety instruments.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount that produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in one or more GAD endpoints when
used according to a regimen disclosed herein.

### 2. Active agents

#### 2.1 Agomelatine

Agomelatine is a melatonergic antidepressant that acts as a potent
agonist at MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C
receptors. In the present invention, its principal contribution is
restoration of sleep continuity, circadian alignment, and overnight
recovery from anxious hyperarousal.

#### 2.2 Vilazodone

Vilazodone is an orally available serotonin reuptake inhibitor and
5-HT1A partial agonist. In the present invention, its principal
contribution is attenuation of worry, anxious distress, and persistent
catastrophic expectancy.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise agomelatine and
vilazodone together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients. The actives may be provided as separate oral
tablets in a co-packaged kit or as a day-night blister configuration
linking vilazodone morning dosing with agomelatine bedtime dosing.

Suitable excipients include common oral-tablet diluents, binders,
disintegrants, lubricants, coating systems, and release-control
materials. In preferred embodiments agomelatine is supplied as an
immediate-release bedtime tablet and vilazodone as an immediate-
release tablet taken with food.

### 4. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with GAD, a preferred regimen comprises:

* Agomelatine 25 mg to 50 mg orally at bedtime.
* Vilazodone 10 mg to 40 mg orally once daily with food.

In certain embodiments vilazodone is titrated from 10 mg to 20 mg and
then to 40 mg as tolerated. In certain embodiments agomelatine is
initiated at 25 mg nightly and escalated to 50 mg nightly after about
2 weeks if clinically indicated and tolerated.

In preferred embodiments the subject's psychotherapy or standard
supportive care is stable so that incremental benefit on anxiety and
sleep endpoints can be meaningfully assessed.

### 5. Subject populations

Preferred subjects are adults with clinician-confirmed GAD and
persistent worry, insomnia, somatic tension, or functional impairment
despite prior psychotherapy, serotonergic treatment, or both. Certain
embodiments focus on subjects whose anxiety is amplified by poor sleep
recovery or nocturnal hyperarousal.

Exclusion criteria for preferred embodiments include active bipolar-
spectrum illness, severe psychosis, acute suicidality requiring
alternate management, severe hepatic impairment incompatible with
agomelatine use, or intolerance to serotonergic agents.

### 6. Mechanistic rationale

GAD often persists because daytime worry and nighttime hyperarousal
reinforce one another. A subject who sleeps poorly enters the next day
with higher autonomic vulnerability and lower emotional resilience,
while a subject with persistent worry may struggle to achieve adequate
overnight recovery.

Vilazodone addresses the daytime worry and catastrophic-expectancy
layer through serotonin reuptake inhibition and 5-HT1A partial
agonism. Agomelatine addresses the sleep-recovery layer through MT1 /
MT2 agonism and 5-HT2C antagonism. The combination is therefore
expected to produce larger improvements in worry burden, somatic
tension, sleep, and daily function than either agent alone at any
individually achievable dose.

### 7. Administration and monitoring

Monitoring may include Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale, GAD-7, sleep
quality assessment, somatic-tension tracking, liver-function tests,
GI tolerability, sexual-side-effect assessment, role function, and
CGI-I.

The regimen is not intended to replace cognitive-behavioral therapy,
exposure to uncertainty-based skills, sleep-hygiene counseling,
evaluation for medical contributors to anxiety, or higher-acuity care
when required.

### 8. Prophetic clinical study

A prophetic Phase 2 study enrolls adults with GAD, baseline elevated
anxiety severity, and persistent sleep disruption or nocturnal
hyperarousal. Subjects are randomized to: (a) agomelatine plus
vilazodone; (b) vilazodone plus placebo; or (c) placebo plus stable
standard of care, for 12 weeks after titration.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in generalized-anxiety
severity. Key secondary endpoints include worry burden, sleep quality,
somatic tension, role function, and CGI-I. The combination arm is
expected to show the largest improvement in both anxiety and sleep-
linked recovery. FIGS. 3-5 are prophetic illustrations of those
expected outcomes.

### 9. Prior-art distinction

The closest prior-art references identified in the prior-art
landscape include:

* Anxiety-related disorder references - disclose general anxiolytic
  treatment concepts but do not teach the specific agomelatine +
  vilazodone combination for GAD.
* Agomelatine and vilazodone formulation references - disclose the
  individual compounds, not the present combination strategy.
* Broader psychiatric combination references - do not teach the
  worry-reduction plus sleep-recovery framework disclosed herein.

The present invention is distinct in the specific receptor classes
combined, the specific actives chosen, and the symptom-layered
strategy of coupling daytime serotonergic worry attenuation with
nighttime melatonergic recovery support in GAD.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Generalized Anxiety Disorder comprising co-administration of a
therapeutically effective amount of the melatonergic MT1 / MT2 agonist
and 5-HT2C antagonist agomelatine and a therapeutically effective
amount of the serotonin reuptake inhibitor / 5-HT1A partial agonist
vilazodone are disclosed. The two agents act on non-overlapping
worry-reduction and sleep-recovery pathways relevant to GAD and are
expected to produce greater-than-additive improvement in anxiety
severity, nocturnal hyperarousal, and daily function compared with
either agent alone at any individually achievable dose.
