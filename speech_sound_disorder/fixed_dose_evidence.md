# Fixed-Dose Evidence Appendix

Application: `110_speech_sound_disorder`
Disorder: Speech Sound Disorder
Active pair: pitolisant + oxytocin
Route feasibility classification: **multi_unit_fixed_regimen**

## Evidence Standard

This appendix uses public-source search links, repository medication metadata, local patents-rag results, and MODELED or PROPHETIC rationale. It does not report completed combination testing, actual fixed-dose stability, actual dissolution, actual PK interaction data, or completed component-contribution results.

## Dose Ratio and Preferred Strengths

Nominal ratio range: 4.5 mg to 35.6 mg pitolisant per 8 IU to 80 IU oxytocin.

| Level | Nominal daily fixed strength | Label |
|---|---|---|
| low | 4.5 mg pitolisant / 8 IU oxytocin | MODELED |
| standard | 20.05 mg pitolisant / 44 IU oxytocin | MODELED |
| high | 35.6 mg pitolisant / 80 IU oxytocin | MODELED |

## Route and Dosage Form

MODELED: the active routes differ (pitolisant: oral; oxytocin: intranasal), so the fixed-dose product is drafted as a fixed labeled regimen with route-specific dosage units in one package rather than as an implausible co-formulated unit.

Product strategy: fixed-dose combination product package with oral pitolisant unit and intranasal oxytocin unit.

## Compatibility, Stability, and Release

MODELED: route-specific units reduce direct chemical contact between actives. Stability support must still be generated for the assembled kit, labeling, container closure, and each constituent product under proposed storage conditions.

MODELED route-specific release: pitolisant follows the oral performance standard and oxytocin follows the intranasal performance standard; no co-dissolution or single-matrix release is asserted.

Stability status: DATA NEEDED. Required studies include forced degradation, accelerated and long-term stability, assay and impurity methods, container-closure compatibility, and in-use testing where applicable.

## Manufacturability

- MODELED: manufacture or source a fixed-strength oral dosage unit for pitolisant
- MODELED: manufacture or source a fixed-strength intranasal dosage unit for oxytocin
- MODELED: assemble route-specific units in a single blister, carton, tray, or labeled kit
- MODELED: include fixed regimen instructions, timing controls, contraindication screening, and discontinuation criteria
- MODELED: qualify container-closure and shipping conditions for each constituent unit

## Component Contribution

pitolisant: MODELED: pitolisant supplies the selective histamine H3 receptor inverse agonist mechanism axis for the Speech Sound Disorder regimen.

oxytocin: MODELED: oxytocin supplies the oxytocin receptor (OXTR) agonist (cyclic nonapeptide) mechanism axis for the Speech Sound Disorder regimen.

PROPHETIC: evaluate fixed-dose pitolisant + oxytocin against pitolisant alone, oxytocin alone, placebo or stable standard care, and a dose-ratio arm in subjects with confirmed Speech Sound Disorder; predefine primary endpoint, safety hierarchy, PK sampling, adherence assessment, and criteria for component contribution.

## Safety and Interaction Controls

- pitolisant: QT prolongation; CYP2D6 interactions
- oxytocin: headache; transient bradycardia; uterine contraction (parenteral)
- pitolisant metabolism/clearance: CYP2D6 / CYP3A4
- oxytocin metabolism/clearance: peptidase clearance
- QT-prolongation and interacting-medication review are required when a QT-sensitive active is present
- route-specific administration errors are mitigated by unit-of-use packaging and fixed labeled timing instructions
