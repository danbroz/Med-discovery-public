# Fixed-Dose Evidence Appendix

Application: `055_brief_psychotic_disorder`
Disorder: Brief Psychotic Disorder
Active pair: xanomeline + perospirone
Route feasibility classification: **single_form_fdc**

## Evidence Standard

This appendix uses public-source search links, repository medication metadata, local patents-rag results, and MODELED or PROPHETIC rationale. It does not report completed combination testing, actual fixed-dose stability, actual dissolution, actual PK interaction data, or completed component-contribution results.

## Dose Ratio and Preferred Strengths

Nominal ratio range: 50 mg to 250 mg xanomeline per 4 mg to 48 mg perospirone.

| Level | Nominal daily fixed strength | Label |
|---|---|---|
| low | 50 mg xanomeline / 4 mg perospirone | MODELED |
| standard | 150 mg xanomeline / 26 mg perospirone | MODELED |
| high | 250 mg xanomeline / 48 mg perospirone | MODELED |

## Route and Dosage Form

MODELED: both repository route records resolve to oral; a single physical fixed-dose dosage form is feasible in principle, subject to compatibility, stability, release, and bioavailability data.

Product strategy: single physical oral fixed-dose tablet or hard capsule.

## Compatibility, Stability, and Release

MODELED: same-route dosing permits a co-formulated dosage form in principle. Actual chemical compatibility, impurity growth, moisture sensitivity, photostability, and excipient compatibility are not asserted and remain DATA NEEDED.

MODELED immediate-release, bilayer, or multiparticulate release profile selected to preserve each active's known administration timing and food effect.

Stability status: DATA NEEDED. Required studies include forced degradation, accelerated and long-term stability, assay and impurity methods, container-closure compatibility, and in-use testing where applicable.

## Manufacturability

- MODELED: direct compression after separate active-excipient compatibility screening
- MODELED: wet granulation with moisture and impurity controls if direct compression fails
- MODELED: bilayer compression when chemical contact, timing, or food-effect constraints favor separated layers
- MODELED: hard-capsule fill using separate granules, pellets, or mini-tablets for each active
- MODELED: blistered low, standard, and high fixed strengths with content-uniformity release testing

## Component Contribution

xanomeline: MODELED: xanomeline supplies the M1/M4-preferring muscarinic acetylcholine receptor agonist mechanism axis for the Brief Psychotic Disorder regimen.

perospirone: MODELED: perospirone supplies the D2/5-HT2A antagonist (atypical antipsychotic) mechanism axis for the Brief Psychotic Disorder regimen.

PROPHETIC: evaluate fixed-dose xanomeline + perospirone against xanomeline alone, perospirone alone, placebo or stable standard care, and a dose-ratio arm in subjects with confirmed Brief Psychotic Disorder; predefine primary endpoint, safety hierarchy, PK sampling, adherence assessment, and criteria for component contribution.

## Safety and Interaction Controls

- xanomeline: cholinergic GI effects (mitigated by trospium)
- perospirone: EPS; hyperprolactinemia; weight gain
- xanomeline metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4 / CYP2D6
- perospirone metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4
