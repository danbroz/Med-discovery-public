# Fixed-Dose Evidence Appendix

Application: `027_hypersomnolence_disorder`
Disorder: Hypersomnolence Disorder
Active pair: pitolisant + Nag 26
Route feasibility classification: **high_risk_development_asset**

## Evidence Standard

This appendix uses public-source search links, repository medication metadata, local patents-rag results, and MODELED or PROPHETIC rationale. It does not report completed combination testing, actual fixed-dose stability, actual dissolution, actual PK interaction data, or completed component-contribution results.

Product strategy: development-asset fixed-dose combination product package.

## Component Contribution

pitolisant: MODELED: pitolisant supplies the selective histamine H3 receptor inverse agonist mechanism axis for the Hypersomnolence Disorder regimen.

Nag 26: MODELED: Nag 26 supplies the selective orexin (OX2) receptor agonist (non-peptide research compound) mechanism axis for the Hypersomnolence Disorder regimen.

PROPHETIC: evaluate fixed-dose pitolisant + Nag 26 against pitolisant alone, Nag 26 alone, placebo or stable standard care, and a dose-ratio arm in subjects with confirmed Hypersomnolence Disorder; predefine primary endpoint, safety hierarchy, PK sampling, adherence assessment, and criteria for component contribution.

## Safety and Interaction Controls

- pitolisant: QT prolongation; CYP2D6 interactions
- Nag 26: no clinical safety database
- pitolisant metabolism/clearance: CYP2D6 / CYP3A4
- Nag 26 metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4 (predicted)
- QT-prolongation and interacting-medication review are required when a QT-sensitive active is present
- development-asset toxicology, human exposure, and abuse or CNS safety margins are DATA NEEDED before clinical use
