## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Lewy Body Disease by co-administering
(a) a histamine H3 receptor antagonist or inverse agonist and
(b) a muscarinic M1/M4 receptor agonist, in particular the
combination of pitolisant and xanomeline, optionally further with a
peripherally restricted antimuscarinic such as trospium to mitigate
peripheral cholinergic adverse effects. The invention addresses
fluctuating attention, daytime alertness, executive dysfunction,
and caregiver burden in Lewy body spectrum neurodegeneration. The
invention is not intended to replace management of parkinsonism,
hallucinations, or autonomic instability, and does not assert
disease-modification of alpha-synuclein pathology.

## Background of the invention

Lewy Body Disease is recognized in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 as a
neurodegenerative disorder characterized by progressive cognitive
decline with core clinical features such as fluctuating cognition
with pronounced variations in attention and alertness, recurrent
well-formed visual hallucinations, parkinsonism, REM sleep behavior
disorder, and neuropsychiatric vulnerability. In many patients, the
dominant disabling syndrome includes fluctuations in arousal,
slowed cognition, and executive inefficiency together with high
caregiver burden.

The prevalence of Lewy body disease is clinically meaningful among
older adults, and the burden is substantial because of cognitive
fluctuation, fall risk, hospitalization risk, caregiver stress, and
sensitivity to many antipsychotic drugs. D2-blocking agents can
cause severe adverse reactions in this population, making
non-dopamine-blocking symptomatic strategies especially valuable.

The current standard of care is supportive and limited. It consists
primarily of cholinesterase inhibitors, sleep management, selective
use of psychosis-targeted therapies, caregiver support, and symptom-
directed management of parkinsonism and autonomic dysfunction. No
pharmaceutical product is approved by the United States Food and
Drug Administration (FDA) specifically for the broad symptomatic
combination of fluctuating attention and daytime arousal impairment
in Lewy body disease. A clinically meaningful gap remains for a
regimen that supports wakefulness and cortical cholinergic signal
quality without D2 blockade.

The mechanistic gap that the present invention addresses is the
absence of a defensible, mechanism-based symptomatic regimen for
the mixed fluctuation-plus-cognitive phenotype of Lewy body
disease. A single-agent approach often leaves one dimension
undertreated: a purely wake-promoting intervention may not restore
cortical signal quality, while a purely pro-cholinergic
intervention may not adequately address severe daytime fluctuation
and hypoarousal.

The present invention recognizes that two non-overlapping CNS axes
can be combined to support cognition and daytime function in Lewy
body disease. First, the histamine H3 receptor is a presynaptic
auto- and heteroreceptor that suppresses histamine release and
modulates acetylcholine, dopamine, and norepinephrine transmission.
Selective inverse agonism at H3 by pitolisant elevates
extracellular histamine and supports wakefulness, attention, and
state stability. The pitolisant axis therefore addresses the
*fluctuating attention and vigilance instability* substrate of
Lewy body disease.

Second, the muscarinic M1 receptor facilitates cortical and
hippocampal cognition, while M4 signaling contributes to behavioral
stabilization. Xanomeline is an M1/M4-preferring muscarinic agonist
developed in the 1990s and more recently formulated with the
peripherally restricted antimuscarinic trospium as the FDA-approved
xanomeline-trospium combination (Cobenfy®) for schizophrenia in
adults. The xanomeline axis therefore addresses the *cortical-
hippocampal cholinergic cognitive-support substrate* of Lewy body
disease.

No issued patent or published application known to the present
inventors discloses the use of an H3 antagonist or inverse agonist
in combination with a muscarinic M1/M4 agonist for Lewy body
disease specifically.

The present invention recognizes that vigilance- and arousal-
enabled cognitive support (a pitolisant H3 axis) and post-synaptic
muscarinic facilitation of cortical cognition (a xanomeline M1/M4
axis) are complementary and convergent substrates for treatment of
Lewy body disease. The combination is expected to produce greater
symptomatic benefit in attention fluctuation, executive function,
and caregiver burden than either agent alone at any individually
achievable dose, while avoiding direct D2 blockade.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) pitolisant or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof, (b) xanomeline or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, and (c) optionally a peripherally restricted
antimuscarinic agent such as trospium chloride in an amount
effective to mitigate peripheral cholinergic adverse effects,
together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable carriers or
excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Lewy Body Disease in a human subject in need thereof, comprising
co-administering to the subject a therapeutically effective amount
of pitolisant and a therapeutically effective amount of xanomeline,
optionally further with a peripherally restricted antimuscarinic.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) pitolisant or a salt thereof in a first unitary dosage form,
(b) xanomeline or a salt thereof, optionally co-formulated with
trospium, in a second unitary dosage form, and (c) written
instructions for the symptomatic treatment of Lewy Body Disease
according to a dosing regimen disclosed herein.

The invention contemplates pitolisant in a daily dose of about
4.5 mg to about 35.6 mg administered orally once daily. The
invention contemplates xanomeline in a daily dose of about 50 mg to
about 250 mg administered orally as the free base or as the tartrate
salt in two divided doses; when xanomeline is co-administered with
trospium chloride, the trospium daily dose is about 40 mg to about
60 mg administered orally in two divided doses.

The technical effect of the combination is improvement, in subjects
with Lewy body disease, in fluctuating attention, executive
function, daytime alertness, and caregiver burden, greater than
would be obtained with either agent alone at any individually
achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Pitolisant"* means the histamine H3 receptor antagonist / inverse
agonist marketed as Wakix®. The term includes pitolisant free base,
hydrochloride or other pharmaceutically acceptable salts,
solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, isotopically labeled
forms, and pharmacologically equivalent H3 inverse agonists.

*"Xanomeline"* means the compound 3-[4-hexyloxy-1,2,5-thiadiazol-3-
yl]-1,2,5,6-tetrahydro-1-methylpyridine, an M1/M4-preferring
muscarinic agonist. The term as used herein includes the free base,
the tartrate salt and other pharmaceutically acceptable salts,
solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, isotopically labeled
forms, and metabolically equivalent analogues.

*"Trospium chloride"* or *"trospium"* means the quaternary ammonium
peripherally restricted muscarinic antagonist that does not
appreciably cross the blood-brain barrier and may be used to
mitigate peripheral cholinergic adverse effects of xanomeline.

*"Lewy Body Disease"* means a neurodegenerative syndrome with core
clinical features including fluctuating attention and alertness,
visual hallucinations, parkinsonism, REM sleep behavior disorder,
or related synucleinopathy-linked cognitive impairment.

*"Attention-fluctuation composite"* means a clinically meaningful
burden of fluctuation in attention, alertness, executive function,
or day-to-day cognitive stability quantified by structured
clinician assessment or validated cognitive / fluctuation
instruments.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount of an active
agent which, when administered to a human subject for the treatment
of Lewy body disease according to a dosing regimen disclosed herein,
produces a clinically meaningful improvement in at least one of:
attention-fluctuation composite, executive-function score, daytime
alertness, caregiver burden, functional fluctuation index, or
investigator-rated CGI-I.

### 2. The active agents

#### 2.1 Pitolisant

Pitolisant is a histamine H3 receptor antagonist / inverse agonist
that increases histaminergic wake drive and supports state-
dependent release of acetylcholine and dopamine. In the present
invention, the principal mechanistic contribution of pitolisant is
attenuation of hypoarousal, fluctuating attention, and daytime
sleepiness in Lewy body disease.

#### 2.2 Xanomeline

Xanomeline is an M1/M4-preferring muscarinic agonist. In the
present invention, the principal mechanistic contribution of
xanomeline is support of cortical cognition and attentional signal
quality without direct D2 antagonism.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise pitolisant and
xanomeline, together with at least one pharmaceutically acceptable
excipient. The compositions may take the form of a co-packaged kit
of a pitolisant tablet and a xanomeline or xanomeline-trospium
dosage form.

Excipients useful for the pitolisant composition include diluents,
binders, disintegrants, lubricants, and coating polymers suitable
for oral tablet manufacture. Excipients useful for the xanomeline
composition include diluents, binders, disintegrants, lubricants,
and stabilizing excipients appropriate for muscarinic agonist oral
delivery.

### 4. Dosage forms

Pitolisant is preferably formulated as an oral tablet suitable for
morning administration. Xanomeline is preferably formulated as an
oral immediate-release or modified-release dosage form administered
in divided doses. The preferred commercial-development embodiment
contemplates xanomeline co-administered with trospium to mitigate
peripheral cholinergic adverse effects.

A kit form of the invention comprises (a) a 30-day supply of
pitolisant tablets, (b) a 30-day supply of xanomeline or
xanomeline-trospium units, and (c) written instructions aligned to
caregiver support, fluctuation tracking, and specialist follow-up.

### 5. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with Lewy body disease, the preferred dosing
regimen is:

* Pitolisant 4.5 mg orally once daily, titratable to 17.8 mg and
  then to 35.6 mg once daily if clinically indicated and tolerated.
* Xanomeline 50 mg orally twice daily, titratable to 100 mg or
  125 mg twice daily if clinically indicated and tolerated; when
  paired with trospium, trospium is administered at about 20 mg to
  30 mg twice daily.

The regimen is not intended to replace management of parkinsonism,
visual hallucinations requiring other treatment, orthostatic
instability, or autonomic complications. Because Lewy body disease
patients may be sensitive to many neuropsychiatric drugs, the
non-D2-blocking nature of this pair is a key practical feature.

### 6. Subject populations

The methods of the present invention are directed to subjects with
Lewy body disease. Inclusion criteria for the preferred treatment
population include: age >= 18 years; clinically meaningful
fluctuating attention, executive dysfunction, or daytime hypoarousal
in a Lewy body syndrome; and availability of caregiver or clinician
monitoring. Exclusion criteria include unstable autonomic failure,
severe cholinergic intolerance, unsafe cardiac conduction
abnormality, and known hypersensitivity to either active.

### 7. Mechanistic rationale

Lewy body disease often includes two interacting layers: fluctuating
vigilance / alertness and reduced cortical cholinergic signal
quality, both of which impair attention and executive function.

Pitolisant addresses the vigilance-fluctuation layer through H3
antagonism / inverse agonism. Xanomeline addresses the cholinergic-
cognition layer through M1/M4 agonism. The two axes are non-
overlapping at the receptor level and converge on improved
attention and daytime functioning without D2 blockade.

Co-administration is therefore expected to produce greater-than-
additive reduction in Lewy body disease cognitive fluctuation than
combination, derived from the master receptor-efficacy summary
(`all_disorders_taxonomy_aware_master_summary.csv`), is 28,
indicating mechanistic compatibility above the reporting threshold
for the indication.

### 8. Pre-clinical support

In-vitro assays useful for confirming the mechanistic basis of the
invention include: H3 receptor binding and inverse-agonism assays
for pitolisant; M1 and M4 receptor binding and signaling assays for
xanomeline; and combined attention / fluctuation network assays
where available.

In-vivo paradigms appropriate for probing the relevant substrates
include synucleinopathy-associated cognitive fluctuation models,
executive-function tasks, wake-maintenance paradigms, and caregiver-
burden proxy paradigms. A supra-additive effect of the combination
on restoration of attention and daytime stability, relative to
either monotherapy at any individually achievable dose, is the
principal in-vivo proof point that motivates the Phase 2 design in
§ 9.

### 9. Clinical study designs

#### 9.1 Phase 1 — pharmacokinetics and medication-medication interaction

A single-center, open-label, three-period, fixed-sequence study in
24 healthy adult volunteers (age 18-55 years). Period 1:
pitolisant 17.8 mg/day for 7 days; Period 2: xanomeline or
xanomeline-trospium for 7 days; Period 3: the combination for
7 days. Full PK sampling, ECG monitoring, orthostatic vital signs,
and tolerability monitoring are obtained.

#### 9.2 Phase 2 — randomized, double-blind, three-arm study

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study of
the combination in adult Lewy body disease (FIG. 4). Three arms,
1:1:1 randomization, target N = 150 (50 per arm).

* Arm A (combination): pitolisant + xanomeline.
* Arm B (active monotherapy): pitolisant + placebo for xanomeline.
* Arm C (placebo): placebo for both, with supportive standard care.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in attention-
fluctuation composite at week 12. Secondary endpoints include
executive-function score, daytime alertness, caregiver burden,
functional fluctuation index, and CGI-I.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of Lewy
Body Disease comprising co-administration of a therapeutically
effective amount of pitolisant and a therapeutically effective
amount of xanomeline are disclosed, optionally with trospium to
mitigate peripheral cholinergic adverse effects. The two agents act
on non-overlapping arousal-support and cortical-cholinergic
circuits and are expected to produce greater-than-additive
improvement in fluctuating attention, daytime alertness, and
caregiver burden than either agent alone at any individually
achievable dose.
