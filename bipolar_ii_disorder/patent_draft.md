## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Bipolar II Disorder by co-administering
(a) a melatonergic MT1 / MT2 receptor agonist with 5-HT2C antagonist
activity and (b) a dopamine D2 / 5-HT2A antagonist atypical
antipsychotic, in particular the combination of agomelatine and
perospirone. The invention is directed to subjects with bipolar II
depression, inter-episode instability, circadian dysregulation, mixed
anxious activation, or recurrent hypomanic cycling, and is not
intended to replace higher-acuity management of severe mania,
frank psychosis, or psychiatric emergency.

## Background of the invention

Bipolar II Disorder is a chronic mood disorder characterized by major
depressive episodes together with hypomanic episodes that do not reach
the severity of full manic episodes seen in bipolar I disorder. The
burden of illness is substantial because subjects often spend a large
proportion of time in bipolar depression, dysphoric activation,
ruminative insomnia, or functionally destabilizing mood cycling rather
than in clear euthymia.

Current care is heterogeneous and frequently unsatisfactory. Standard
approaches may include mood stabilizers, atypical antipsychotics,
psychotherapy, sleep regularization, and cautious use of
antidepressant-adjacent strategies. However, persistent circadian
flattening, poor sleep continuity, depressive inertia, mixed anxious
activation, and recurrent subsyndromal hypomanic shifts remain common.
There remains a need for a mechanism-based combination that addresses
both the circadian-depressive substrate and the dopamine-salience /
activation substrate of bipolar II illness.

The mechanistic gap addressed by the present invention has two layers.
First, agomelatine is a melatonergic antidepressant that acts as an
agonist at MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C
receptors. This pharmacology supports circadian resynchronization,
sleep restoration, and disinhibition of frontal dopaminergic and
noradrenergic tone relevant to depressive inertia. Second, perospirone
is an atypical antipsychotic with D2 antagonism, 5-HT2A antagonism,
and 5-HT1A partial agonist activity. In the present invention, the
perospirone axis provides a counterbalance to hypomanic activation,
mixed irritability, and mood-linked dopaminergic over-salience.

The inventors recognized that these mechanisms are complementary in
bipolar II disorder. Agomelatine addresses the circadian and
sleep-linked depressive layer that often dominates time ill, while
perospirone provides antipsychotic / antimanic stabilization without
requiring reliance solely on sedative escalation. The combination is
therefore expected to reduce depressive burden, mixed activation, and
cycling-related instability more effectively than either monotherapy
at any individually achievable dose.

Prior-art searching identified *Use of agomelatine in obtaining
medicaments intended for the treatment of bipolar disorders* and other
bipolar references, including antimanic disclosures and general
combination-therapy references. Those references do not teach the
specific combination of agomelatine and perospirone for Bipolar II
Disorder, nor the circadian-stabilization plus activation-containment
framework disclosed herein.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) agomelatine or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, co-crystal, or prodrug thereof and
(b) perospirone or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate,
polymorph, or prodrug thereof, together with one or more
pharmaceutically acceptable carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Bipolar II Disorder in a human subject in need thereof, comprising
co-administering a therapeutically effective amount of agomelatine and
a therapeutically effective amount of perospirone. In preferred
embodiments, agomelatine is administered at bedtime and perospirone is
administered once or twice daily depending on symptom phenotype and
tolerability.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) agomelatine in a first dosage form, (b) perospirone in a second
dosage form, and (c) written instructions for use in subjects with
Bipolar II Disorder, including guidance regarding sleep regularity,
mood monitoring, switch surveillance, and continued psychotherapy or
other standard mood-disorder care.

The invention contemplates agomelatine in a daily dose of about
25 mg to about 50 mg, preferably administered orally at bedtime. The
invention contemplates perospirone in a daily dose of about 4 mg to
about 48 mg, preferably about 8 mg to about 24 mg per day in one or
two divided doses.

The technical effect of the combination is expected to be greater than
additive improvement in one or more endpoints selected from the group
consisting of bipolar-depression severity, sleep continuity, mixed-
activation burden, hypomanic-episode frequency, role function, and
Clinical Global Impression-Improvement (CGI-I), compared with either
monotherapy at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Bipolar II Disorder"* means bipolar II disorder as defined by
DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent diagnostic framework, including
subjects with recurrent major depressive episodes and at least one
hypomanic episode without a history of a full manic episode.

*"Agomelatine"* means the melatonergic antidepressant
N-[2-(7-methoxy-1-naphthyl)ethyl]acetamide and pharmaceutically
acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, co-crystals,
prodrugs, and metabolically equivalent forms that retain clinically
relevant MT1 / MT2 agonism and 5-HT2C antagonism.

*"Perospirone"* means the atypical antipsychotic marketed in Japan for
schizophrenia and includes the free base, pharmaceutically acceptable
salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, labeled forms, and
metabolically equivalent analogues that retain clinically relevant D2
and 5-HT2A antagonism.

*"Mixed-activation burden"* means a clinically meaningful burden of
agitation, irritability, racing thoughts, reduced need for sleep,
inner restlessness, or mood-state volatility occurring in the context
of bipolar II illness.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount that produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in one or more bipolar II endpoints
when used according to a regimen disclosed herein.

### 2. Active agents

#### 2.1 Agomelatine

Agomelatine is a melatonergic antidepressant that acts as a potent
agonist at MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2C
receptors. In the present invention, its principal contribution is
restoration of circadian alignment, improvement in sleep continuity,
and reduction of depressive inertia.

#### 2.2 Perospirone

Perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic with D2 antagonism, 5-HT2A
antagonism, and 5-HT1A partial agonist activity. In the present
invention, its principal contribution is attenuation of hypomanic
activation, mixed irritability, and mood-linked salience dyscontrol.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise agomelatine and
perospirone together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients. The actives may be provided as separate oral
tablets or capsules, as a co-packaged kit, or as a day-night blister
card that links bedtime agomelatine use with daytime or divided
perospirone use.

Suitable excipients include common diluents, binders, disintegrants,
lubricants, coating systems, and release-control excipients
appropriate for oral dosage forms. In preferred embodiments the
agomelatine component is administered at bedtime and the perospirone
component is titrated according to activation burden and tolerability.

### 4. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with Bipolar II Disorder, a preferred regimen
comprises:

* Agomelatine 25 mg to 50 mg orally at bedtime.
* Perospirone 4 mg to 48 mg per day, preferably 8 mg to 24 mg per day
  in one or two divided doses.

In certain embodiments agomelatine is initiated first to regularize
sleep and circadian timing, and perospirone is added or titrated when
hypomanic activation, mixed irritability, or recurrent cycling remains
prominent. In other embodiments perospirone is initiated first in
subjects with unstable activation and agomelatine is subsequently
layered in after initial stabilization.

In preferred embodiments the subject's background psychotherapy or
mood-stabilizing care is stable so that the symptomatic effect of the
present combination can be meaningfully assessed.

### 5. Subject populations

Preferred subjects are adults with clinician-confirmed Bipolar II
Disorder, especially subjects with depressive predominance, recurrent
insomnia or circadian irregularity, mixed anxious activation, or
frequent subsyndromal hypomanic cycling. Certain embodiments target
subjects who have had inadequate response or limited tolerability with
prior mood-stabilizing or antidepressant-based regimens.

Exclusion criteria for preferred embodiments include acute severe
mania, frank psychosis requiring higher-acuity antipsychotic or
hospital care, active suicidal crisis requiring alternate management,
intolerance to dopamine antagonists, or severe hepatic impairment that
would make agomelatine use inappropriate.

### 6. Mechanistic rationale

Bipolar II illness often combines a depressive-circadian layer and an
activation-salience layer. The depressive-circadian layer includes
insomnia, circadian flattening, poor overnight recovery, and mood
inertia. The activation-salience layer includes irritability, racing
thoughts, reduced need for sleep, and hypomanic or mixed-state
destabilization.

Agomelatine addresses the depressive-circadian layer through MT1 / MT2
agonism and 5-HT2C antagonism. Perospirone addresses the activation-
salience layer through D2 / 5-HT2A antagonism with additional 5-HT1A
partial agonist support. The combination is therefore expected to
produce larger improvements in bipolar II depression and cycling
stability than either agent alone at any individually achievable dose.

### 7. Administration and monitoring

Monitoring may include bipolar-depression rating scales, sleep diary,
Young Mania Rating Scale or hypomania burden assessment, mixed-
activation tracking, liver-function tests, extrapyramidal symptom
monitoring, prolactin-related tolerability assessment, and CGI-I.

The regimen is not intended to replace psychiatric emergency care,
management of severe mania, hospitalization when necessary, or
longitudinal psychotherapy and psychosocial support that remain
essential in bipolar disorder care.

### 8. Prophetic clinical study

A prophetic Phase 2 study enrolls adults with Bipolar II Disorder,
baseline bipolar-depression symptoms, and at least one recent period
of insomnia, mixed activation, or cycling instability. Subjects are
randomized to: (a) agomelatine plus perospirone; (b) perospirone plus
placebo; or (c) placebo plus stable standard of care, for 12 weeks
after titration.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in bipolar-depression
severity. Key secondary endpoints include sleep continuity, mixed-
activation burden, hypomanic-episode frequency, role function, and
CGI-I. The combination arm is expected to show the largest
improvement in depressive burden with lower sleep-related and cycling-
related instability than comparator arms. FIGS. 3-5 are prophetic
illustrations of those expected outcomes.

### 9. Prior-art distinction

The closest prior-art references identified in the prior-art
landscape include:

* *Use of agomelatine in obtaining medicaments intended for the
  treatment of bipolar disorders* - discloses agomelatine in bipolar
  illness generally, but does not teach perospirone co-administration.
* General bipolar-treatment and antimanic references - disclose
  different agents or treatment frameworks, not the specific
  agomelatine + perospirone pairing.
* General psychiatric combination-therapy references - do not teach
  the circadian-stabilization plus activation-containment framework
  disclosed herein.

The present invention is distinct in the specific receptor classes
combined, the specific actives chosen, and the framing of bipolar II
illness as a condition requiring both circadian-depressive support and
activation control.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of Bipolar
II Disorder comprising co-administration of a therapeutically
effective amount of the melatonergic MT1 / MT2 agonist and 5-HT2C
antagonist agomelatine and a therapeutically effective amount of the
atypical antipsychotic perospirone are disclosed. The two agents act
on non-overlapping circadian-depressive and activation-control
pathways relevant to bipolar II illness and are expected to produce
greater-than-additive improvement in depressive burden, sleep
continuity, and cycling stability compared with either agent alone at
any individually achievable dose.
