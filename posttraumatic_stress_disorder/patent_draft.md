## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) by
co-administering (a) a corticotropin-releasing hormone receptor type 1
(CRHR1) antagonist and (b) an alpha-1 adrenergic receptor antagonist,
in particular the combination of crinecerfont and silodosin, in
dosage forms and on schedules selected to reduce trauma-related
hyperarousal, nightmares, sleep fragmentation, and intrusive symptoms.

## Background of the invention

PTSD is a trauma- and stressor-related disorder characterized by
intrusive recollections, avoidance, negative alterations in mood and
cognition, and persistent hyperarousal after exposure to actual or
threatened death, serious injury, or sexual violence. Sleep
disturbance, trauma-related nightmares, autonomic overactivation, and
exaggerated startle responses are common and often treatment-limiting.
PTSD can be chronic, disabling, and highly comorbid with depression,
substance use disorders, chronic pain, and suicidality.

The current standard of care relies on trauma-focused psychotherapy
and, when medication is used, predominantly serotonergic agents such
as selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors. A subset of patients
benefits from alpha-1 antagonists such as prazosin for trauma-related
nightmares, but clinical response is inconsistent and tolerability can
be limited by orthostasis or titration burden. There remains an unmet
need for a rational combination therapy that addresses both the
upstream stress-axis biology of PTSD and the downstream adrenergic
hyperarousal phenotype that drives nightmares and sleep disruption.

The inventors recognized that PTSD involves at least two partially
separable neurobiologic layers. One layer is stress-axis overdrive,
with excessive corticotropin-releasing hormone signaling and
maladaptive hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) activation. A second
layer is persistent adrenergic hyperarousal, especially during the
night, contributing to nightmares, sleep fragmentation, and autonomic
symptoms. Crinecerfont, a selective CRHR1 antagonist, addresses the
stress-axis layer. Silodosin, a selective alpha-1A adrenergic receptor
antagonist selected in the prazosin class for PTSD-related nightmares,
addresses the adrenergic hyperarousal layer with once-daily dosing.

The present invention is based on the recognition that these
mechanisms are complementary rather than redundant. CRHR1 antagonism
reduces the pathologic amplification of stress signaling, while
alpha-1 antagonism reduces symptomatic adrenergic expression,
particularly during the sleep interval. By engaging both axes, the
combination is expected to reduce nightmare burden, improve sleep
continuity, lower hyperarousal, and improve overall PTSD symptom
severity more effectively than either monotherapy at any individually
achievable dose.

Prior-art searching identified PTSD-focused disclosures such as
*Methods for treating post traumatic stress disorder* and
*Methods and compositions for treating symptoms associated with
post-traumatic stress disorder using cyclobenzaprine*, as well as
other stress-pathway references. However, the inventors are unaware of
any issued patent or published application teaching the specific
combination of crinecerfont and silodosin for PTSD, nor the symptom-
layered rationale and dosing relationship disclosed herein.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) crinecerfont or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof and (b) silodosin or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating PTSD in
a human subject in need thereof, comprising co-administering a
therapeutically effective amount of crinecerfont and a therapeutically
effective amount of silodosin. In preferred embodiments, crinecerfont
is administered twice daily and silodosin is administered once daily
in the evening or at bedtime.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) crinecerfont capsules in a first dosage form, (b) silodosin
capsules in a second dosage form, and (c) written instructions for use
in subjects with PTSD, including guidance for orthostatic monitoring,
nightmare-frequency tracking, and continued psychotherapy or other
standard-of-care support.

The invention contemplates crinecerfont in a daily dose of about
50 mg to about 200 mg, preferably in divided twice-daily dosing. The
invention contemplates silodosin in a daily dose of about 4 mg to
about 8 mg, preferably administered in the evening or at bedtime.

The technical effect of the combination is expected to be greater than
additive improvement in one or more endpoints selected from the group
consisting of CAPS-5 total score, PCL-5 total score, trauma-related
nightmare frequency, sleep continuity, nighttime awakening burden,
CGI-I, and daytime hyperarousal symptoms, compared with either
monotherapy at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Posttraumatic Stress Disorder"* or *"PTSD"* means PTSD as defined by
DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent diagnostic framework, including
subjects whose prominent symptom burden includes nightmares, sleep
disturbance, autonomic hyperarousal, or intrusive trauma recollection.

*"Crinecerfont"* means the selective CRHR1 antagonist known as
crinecerfont and includes pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates,
hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, isotopically labeled forms, and
metabolically equivalent analogues that retain clinically relevant
CRHR1 antagonism.

*"Silodosin"* means the selective alpha-1A adrenergic receptor
antagonist known as silodosin and includes pharmaceutically acceptable
salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, and metabolically
equivalent forms that retain clinically relevant alpha-1 antagonism.

*"Nightmare burden"* means the frequency, severity, distress, or sleep
disruption associated with trauma-related dreams, nocturnal panic, or
recurrent awakenings linked to traumatic memory processing.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount that produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in one or more PTSD endpoints when
used according to a regimen disclosed herein.

### 2. Active agents

#### 2.1 Crinecerfont

Crinecerfont is a selective CRHR1 antagonist. In the present
invention, its principal contribution is attenuation of pathologic
stress-axis signaling and reduction of sustained trauma-associated
arousal pressure.

#### 2.2 Silodosin

Silodosin is a selective alpha-1A adrenergic receptor antagonist
selected here as a long-half-life alpha-1 antagonist in the prazosin
class for trauma-related nightmares and hyperarousal. In the present
invention, its principal contribution is reduction of adrenergic
sleep disruption, nighttime awakenings, and trauma-related autonomic
activation.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise crinecerfont and
silodosin together with at least one pharmaceutically acceptable
carrier or excipient. The actives may be provided in separate oral
capsules or tablets, as a co-packaged kit, or as a blister-card
system that links morning and evening dosing intervals.

Suitable excipients include diluents, binders, disintegrants,
lubricants, capsule-fill materials, and coating systems commonly used
for oral solid dosage forms. In preferred embodiments, the
crinecerfont component is formulated for twice-daily oral use and the
silodosin component for once-daily bedtime use.

### 4. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with PTSD, a preferred regimen comprises:

* Crinecerfont 50 mg to 200 mg per day, preferably divided twice
  daily.
* Silodosin 4 mg to 8 mg once daily, preferably in the evening or at
  bedtime.

In certain embodiments, silodosin is initiated at 4 mg nightly and
titrated to 8 mg nightly as tolerated. In certain embodiments,
crinecerfont is initiated first to establish daytime tolerability and
silodosin is layered in when nightmare burden or nocturnal hyperarousal
is a prominent residual symptom cluster.

The dosing relationship may be tailored to symptom phenotype. Subjects
with prominent nightmares and sleep fragmentation may benefit from a
stronger evening silodosin emphasis, whereas subjects with marked
daytime hyperarousal may benefit from optimized crinecerfont exposure
throughout the day.

### 5. Subject populations

Preferred subjects are adults with clinician-confirmed PTSD and
persistent symptom burden despite psychotherapy, serotonergic
pharmacotherapy, or both. Preferred embodiments include subjects with
trauma-related nightmares, sleep disruption, or adrenergic hyperarousal.

In certain embodiments the subject is maintained on a stable trauma-
focused psychotherapy plan, stable antidepressant regimen, or both,
for at least 2 to 4 weeks before initiation of the present
combination, allowing measurement of incremental benefit.

Exclusion criteria for preferred embodiments include uncontrolled
orthostatic hypotension, syncope-prone states, severe untreated sleep
apnea, acute substance withdrawal, active psychosis or mania, and
acute suicidal crisis requiring a higher-acuity intervention.

### 6. Mechanistic rationale

PTSD symptom persistence is driven by both central stress-amplification
biology and peripheral / central adrenergic expression. CRHR1
antagonism by crinecerfont addresses the stress-amplification layer by
reducing the drive that sustains anxious arousal and physiological
re-experiencing. Alpha-1 antagonism by silodosin addresses the
downstream adrenergic expression layer, particularly during sleep,
where trauma-related sympathetic activation contributes to nightmares
and fragmented rest.

By targeting these layers together, the invention is expected to
reduce the nocturnal and daytime symptom cascade more effectively than
either active alone. Better sleep continuity is further expected to
feed forward into lower daytime reactivity and improved therapy
participation.

### 7. Administration and monitoring

Monitoring may include one or more of CAPS-5, PCL-5, nightmare
frequency diary, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, orthostatic vital
signs, nighttime awakening count, and CGI-I. In certain embodiments,
morning cortisol or other stress-axis biomarkers are followed as
exploratory pharmacodynamic measures.

The regimen is not intended to replace trauma-focused psychotherapy,
evidence-based counseling, emergency management of suicidality,
substance-withdrawal treatment, or higher-level care when required.

### 8. Prophetic clinical study

A prophetic Phase 2 study enrolls adults with PTSD, baseline PCL-5
elevation, and at least two trauma-related nightmares per week.
Subjects are randomized to: (a) crinecerfont plus silodosin;
(b) crinecerfont plus placebo; or (c) placebo plus stable standard of
care, for 10 weeks after a brief titration phase.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in trauma-related
nightmare frequency. Key secondary endpoints include CAPS-5 total
score, PCL-5 total score, sleep continuity, CGI-I responder rate, and
daytime hyperarousal. The combination arm is expected to show the
largest reduction in nightmare burden and improved sleep continuity,
with corresponding improvement in broader PTSD severity. FIGS. 3-5 are
prophetic illustrations of those expected outcomes.

### 9. Prior-art distinction

The closest prior-art references identified in the prior-art
landscape include:

* *Methods for treating post traumatic stress disorder* - discloses
  PTSD treatment concepts generally, but does not teach the specific
  combination of crinecerfont and silodosin.
* *Methods and compositions for treating symptoms associated with
  post-traumatic stress disorder using cyclobenzaprine* - discloses a
  different pharmacologic approach and does not teach dual CRHR1 plus
  alpha-1 antagonism.
* Biomarker- or formulation-oriented PTSD references - do not teach
  the symptom-layered combination disclosed herein.

The present invention is distinct in the specific receptor classes
combined, the specific actives chosen, and the dosing relationship
that pairs daytime stress-axis modulation with bedtime alpha-1
nightmare control.

## Examples

### Example 1 - Crinecerfont capsule, 50 mg

A hard-gelatin capsule is filled with 50 mg crinecerfont, lactose,
microcrystalline cellulose, croscarmellose sodium, and magnesium
stearate, and is packaged for twice-daily oral administration.

### Example 2 - Silodosin capsule, 4 mg

A capsule contains 4 mg silodosin with pharmaceutically acceptable
capsule-fill excipients and is labeled for evening administration with
food or at bedtime, with counseling regarding orthostatic monitoring.

### Example 3 - Prophetic Phase 2 outcome

After 10 weeks of treatment, the combination arm is expected to show a
greater reduction in weekly nightmare frequency and better sleep
continuity than either comparator arm, together with clinically
meaningful improvements in CAPS-5 and PCL-5. PROPHETIC.

### Example 4 - Worked patient case

A 41-year-old adult with chronic PTSD, persistent nightmares despite
stable psychotherapy and sertraline, and no syncope history begins
crinecerfont 50 mg twice daily together with silodosin 4 mg at
bedtime, later titrated to 8 mg nightly. After 8 weeks the patient
reports fewer trauma-related awakenings, improved sleep continuity,
less daytime hyperarousal, and CGI-I rated 2 ("much improved").
PROPHETIC.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Posttraumatic Stress Disorder comprising co-administration of a
therapeutically effective amount of the CRHR1 antagonist
crinecerfont and a therapeutically effective amount of the
alpha-1 adrenergic receptor antagonist silodosin are disclosed. The
two agents act on non-overlapping stress-axis and adrenergic
hyperarousal pathways implicated in PTSD and are expected to produce
greater-than-additive improvement in trauma-related nightmares, sleep
continuity, and overall symptom burden compared with either agent
alone at any individually achievable dose. Preferred embodiments
maintain psychotherapy or other standard of care during treatment.
