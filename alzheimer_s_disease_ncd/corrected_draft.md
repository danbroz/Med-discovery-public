## Field of the invention

The invention relates to pharmaceutical methods, compositions, kits, and dosing regimens for treating Alzheimer's Disease NCD by co-administering pitolisant and xanomeline, using a regimen selected to engage H3 antagonism + M1 agonism while maintaining safety monitoring appropriate for the patient population and the pharmacology of the active agents.

## Background of the invention

Alzheimer's Disease NCD is a CNS, psychiatric, neurocognitive, sleep, substance-use, or mental-health condition for which many subjects have residual symptoms, incomplete response, relapse, tolerability limitations, or functional impairment after available standard care.

The active agents pitolisant and xanomeline are selected to address non-identical mechanistic axes: H3 antagonism + M1 agonism. The invention is not based on routine substitution of one known drug for another. It is directed to a defined coadministration regimen, patient-selection framework, endpoint package, and safety-monitoring plan.
