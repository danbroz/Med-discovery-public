# Fixed-dose pharmaceutical products for treating Alzheimer's Disease NCD with pitolisant and xanomeline

## Field of the invention

The invention relates to fixed-dose pharmaceutical products, fixed-dose combination product packages, methods of treatment, kits, dose-ratio embodiments, release-profile embodiments, and manufacturing embodiments for treating Alzheimer's Disease NCD with pitolisant and xanomeline.

## Background of the invention

Alzheimer's Disease NCD is a central-nervous-system, psychiatric, neurocognitive, sleep, substance-use, or mental-health condition with remaining need for better-tolerated, more adherent, and more precisely monitored pharmacologic regimens.

The present disclosure converts a coadministration concept into a fixed-dose medication product posture. The active agents are selected to engage H3 antagonism + M1 agonism. The fixed-dose posture is limited by route feasibility, component contribution, compatibility, stability, release performance, safety monitoring, and written-description support.

This specification does not represent completed combination testing. Statements marked MODELED are based on repository metadata, public-source search links, known pharmacologic context, and drafting inference. Statements marked PROPHETIC describe prospective work. Statements marked DATA NEEDED identify information that must be generated or verified before relying on broader claim scope.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a single physical fixed-dose pharmaceutical product comprising fixed amounts of pitolisant and xanomeline for use in treating Alzheimer's Disease NCD.

The route-feasibility classification for this application is **single_form_fdc**. MODELED: both repository route records resolve to oral; a single physical fixed-dose dosage form is feasible in principle, subject to compatibility, stability, release, and bioavailability data.

In additional aspects, the invention provides methods of treatment, kit instructions, manufacturing routes, stability protocols, dissolution or route-performance protocols, and component-contribution study designs.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

"Alzheimer's Disease NCD" means the disorder as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent clinical diagnostic framework, including only subtypes and severity levels supported by the disclosure.

"Fixed-dose pharmaceutical product" means a product in which predetermined amounts of two active agents are supplied in a single physical dosage form or in route-specific dosage units packaged and labeled for a fixed regimen.

"Single physical dosage form" means a tablet, capsule, film, spray, injection, depot, or other dosage unit in which both active agents are contained in one administered unit.

"Fixed labeled regimen" means written instructions that specify the strength, route, timing, titration, monitoring, contraindication checks, and discontinuation criteria for the packaged active agents.

"MODELED" means a statement derived from computational, repository, mechanistic, public-source, or pharmacologic rationale without completed combination testing.

"PROPHETIC" means a prospective example, study design, manufacturing protocol, or endpoint framework that has not been carried out for the disclosed fixed-dose product.

"DATA NEEDED" means evidence that is not presently represented by this package and must be generated or verified before final filing reliance, clinical use, or non-provisional claim expansion.

### 2. Active agents

Pitolisant is the first active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a selective histamine H3 receptor inverse agonist, with reference route oral, reference dosage form immediate-release tablet, dose context 4.5 to 35.6 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP2D6 / CYP3A4, and safety liability QT prolongation; CYP2D6 interactions.

Xanomeline is the second active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a M1/M4-preferring muscarinic acetylcholine receptor agonist, with reference route oral, reference dosage form twice-daily capsule, co-formulated with peripheral muscarinic antagonist, dose context 50 to 250 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP3A4 / CYP2D6, and safety liability cholinergic GI effects (mitigated by trospium).

A pharmaceutically acceptable salt, hydrate, solvate, polymorph, isotopically labeled form, prodrug, or co-crystal is included only for embodiments with written-description, manufacturing, analytical, and clinical-pharmacology support.

The fixed-dose product strategy is single physical oral fixed-dose tablet or hard capsule.

### 4. Dose ratios and strengths

MODELED: fixed-dose strengths are derived from repository individual-active dose contexts and are not completed combination dose-ranging results.

Nominal ratio range: 4.5 mg to 35.6 mg pitolisant per 50 mg to 250 mg xanomeline.

| Strength level | Nominal daily fixed strength |
|---|---|
| low | 4.5 mg pitolisant / 50 mg xanomeline |
| standard | 20.05 mg pitolisant / 150 mg xanomeline |
| high | 35.6 mg pitolisant / 250 mg xanomeline |

The disclosed strengths may be adjusted for renal function, hepatic function, age, body weight, sex, pharmacogenomic status, interacting medications, route-specific bioavailability, tolerability, and regulatory label constraints.

### 8. Safety, interactions, and monitoring

Administration includes baseline medication review, contraindication screening, adverse-event monitoring, endpoint tracking, and route-specific counseling.

* pitolisant: QT prolongation; CYP2D6 interactions
* xanomeline: cholinergic GI effects (mitigated by trospium)
* pitolisant metabolism/clearance: CYP2D6 / CYP3A4
* xanomeline metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4 / CYP2D6
* QT-prolongation and interacting-medication review are required when a QT-sensitive active is present

### 9. Subject populations

Preferred subjects have confirmed Alzheimer's Disease NCD, a defined baseline endpoint burden, and inadequate response, intolerance, residual symptoms, relapse risk, adherence limitations, or functional impairment after at least one standard-care intervention, unless a protocol justifies first-line investigational use.

Exclusion criteria include high-acuity psychiatric or medical states requiring immediate intervention, contraindications to either active agent, unsafe interacting medications, pregnancy or lactation concerns where relevant, inability to follow route-specific instructions, and inability to complete the monitoring plan.
