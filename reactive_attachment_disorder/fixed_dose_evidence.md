# Fixed-Dose Evidence Appendix

Application: `098_reactive_attachment_disorder`
Disorder: Reactive Attachment Disorder
Active pair: oxytocin + encenicline
Route feasibility classification: **high_risk_development_asset**

## Evidence Standard

This appendix uses public-source search links, repository medication metadata, local patents-rag results, and MODELED or PROPHETIC rationale. It does not report completed combination testing, actual fixed-dose stability, actual dissolution, actual PK interaction data, or completed component-contribution results.

## Route and Dosage Form

MODELED: at least one active is a research or shelved development asset. The package is retained only as a development-asset fixed-dose product with API supply, toxicology, CMC, clinical-pharmacology, and regulatory gaps expressly marked DATA NEEDED.

Product strategy: development-asset fixed-dose combination product package.

## Compatibility, Stability, and Release

DATA NEEDED: development-asset identity, impurity profile, salt/form selection, and clinical formulation are not mature enough to support actual stability assertions.

DATA NEEDED: release and performance cannot be finalized until development-asset API, salt or form, analytical method, toxicology, and clinical route are established.

Stability status: DATA NEEDED. Required studies include forced degradation, accelerated and long-term stability, assay and impurity methods, container-closure compatibility, and in-use testing where applicable.

- DATA NEEDED: assemble only authorized clinical-investigation packages until regulatory and CMC support is added

## Component Contribution

oxytocin: MODELED: oxytocin supplies the oxytocin receptor (OXTR) agonist (cyclic nonapeptide) mechanism axis for the Reactive Attachment Disorder regimen.

encenicline: MODELED: encenicline supplies the selective α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor partial agonist mechanism axis for the Reactive Attachment Disorder regimen.

PROPHETIC: evaluate fixed-dose oxytocin + encenicline against oxytocin alone, encenicline alone, placebo or stable standard care, and a dose-ratio arm in subjects with confirmed Reactive Attachment Disorder; predefine primary endpoint, safety hierarchy, PK sampling, adherence assessment, and criteria for component contribution.

## Safety and Interaction Controls

- oxytocin: headache; transient bradycardia; uterine contraction (parenteral)
- encenicline: GI events that paused development
- oxytocin metabolism/clearance: peptidase clearance
- encenicline metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4
- development-asset toxicology, human exposure, and abuse or CNS safety margins are DATA NEEDED before clinical use
