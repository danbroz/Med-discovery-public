## Field of the invention

The invention relates to fixed-dose pharmaceutical products, fixed-dose combination product packages, methods of treatment, kits, dose-ratio embodiments, release-profile embodiments, and manufacturing embodiments for treating Reactive Attachment Disorder with oxytocin and encenicline.

## Background of the invention

Reactive Attachment Disorder is a central-nervous-system, psychiatric, neurocognitive, sleep, substance-use, or mental-health condition with remaining need for better-tolerated, more adherent, and more precisely monitored pharmacologic regimens.

The present disclosure converts a coadministration concept into a fixed-dose medication product posture. The active agents are selected to engage oxytocin receptor agonism + α7 nicotinic agonism. The fixed-dose posture is limited by route feasibility, component contribution, compatibility, stability, release performance, safety monitoring, and written-description support.

This specification does not represent completed combination testing. Statements marked MODELED are based on repository metadata, public-source search links, known pharmacologic context, and drafting inference. Statements marked PROPHETIC describe prospective work. Statements marked DATA NEEDED identify information that must be generated or verified before relying on broader claim scope.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

"Reactive Attachment Disorder" means the disorder as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent clinical diagnostic framework, including only subtypes and severity levels supported by the disclosure.

"Fixed-dose pharmaceutical product" means a product in which predetermined amounts of two active agents are supplied in a single physical dosage form or in route-specific dosage units packaged and labeled for a fixed regimen.

"Single physical dosage form" means a tablet, capsule, film, spray, injection, depot, or other dosage unit in which both active agents are contained in one administered unit.

"Fixed labeled regimen" means written instructions that specify the strength, route, timing, titration, monitoring, contraindication checks, and discontinuation criteria for the packaged active agents.

"MODELED" means a statement derived from computational, repository, mechanistic, public-source, or pharmacologic rationale without completed combination testing.

"PROPHETIC" means a prospective example, study design, manufacturing protocol, or endpoint framework that has not been carried out for the disclosed fixed-dose product.

"DATA NEEDED" means evidence that is not presently represented by this package and must be generated or verified before final filing reliance, clinical use, or non-provisional claim expansion.

### 2. Active agents

Oxytocin is the first active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a oxytocin receptor (OXTR) agonist (cyclic nonapeptide), with reference route intranasal, reference dosage form metered-dose nasal spray (4 IU/actuation), dose context 8 to 80 IU/day, metabolism or clearance peptidase clearance, and safety liability headache; transient bradycardia; uterine contraction (parenteral).

Encenicline is the second active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a selective α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor partial agonist, with reference route oral, reference dosage form once-daily tablet, dose context 0.3 to 3 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP3A4, and safety liability GI events that paused development.

A pharmaceutically acceptable salt, hydrate, solvate, polymorph, isotopically labeled form, prodrug, or co-crystal is included only for embodiments with written-description, manufacturing, analytical, and clinical-pharmacology support.
