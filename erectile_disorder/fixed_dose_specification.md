# Fixed-dose pharmaceutical products for treating Erectile Disorder with mianserin and testosterone propionate

**U.S. Provisional Patent Application under 35 U.S.C. 111(b)**

## Cross-reference to related applications

None. This specification is prepared as a first-filed U.S. provisional application and does not claim priority to any earlier application.

## Field of the invention

The invention relates to fixed-dose pharmaceutical products, fixed-dose combination product packages, methods of treatment, kits, dose-ratio embodiments, release-profile embodiments, and manufacturing embodiments for treating Erectile Disorder with mianserin and testosterone propionate.

## Background of the invention

Erectile Disorder is a central-nervous-system, psychiatric, neurocognitive, sleep, substance-use, or mental-health condition with remaining need for better-tolerated, more adherent, and more precisely monitored pharmacologic regimens.

The present disclosure converts a coadministration concept into a fixed-dose medication product posture. The active agents are selected to engage HTR2C antagonism + androgen receptor agonism. The fixed-dose posture is limited by route feasibility, component contribution, compatibility, stability, release performance, safety monitoring, and written-description support.

This specification does not represent completed combination testing. Statements marked MODELED are based on repository metadata, public-source search links, known pharmacologic context, and drafting inference. Statements marked PROPHETIC describe prospective work. Statements marked DATA NEEDED identify information that must be generated or verified before relying on broader claim scope.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a multi-unit fixed labeled regimen comprising fixed amounts of mianserin and testosterone propionate for use in treating Erectile Disorder.

The route-feasibility classification for this application is **multi_unit_fixed_regimen**. MODELED: the active routes differ (mianserin: oral; testosterone propionate: intramuscular injection), so the fixed-dose product is drafted as a fixed labeled regimen with route-specific dosage units in one package rather than as an implausible co-formulated unit.

In one embodiment, the product is fixed-dose combination product package with oral mianserin unit and intramuscular injection testosterone propionate unit. Dose strengths are disclosed as low, standard, and high fixed-dose levels derived from individual-active dose contexts, subject to label review, pharmacokinetic review, tolerability review, and component-contribution confirmation.

In additional aspects, the invention provides methods of treatment, kit instructions, manufacturing routes, stability protocols, dissolution or route-performance protocols, and component-contribution study designs.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

"Erectile Disorder" means the disorder as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11, or an equivalent clinical diagnostic framework, including only subtypes and severity levels supported by the disclosure.

"Fixed-dose pharmaceutical product" means a product in which predetermined amounts of two active agents are supplied in a single physical dosage form or in route-specific dosage units packaged and labeled for a fixed regimen.

"Single physical dosage form" means a tablet, capsule, film, spray, injection, depot, or other dosage unit in which both active agents are contained in one administered unit.

"Fixed labeled regimen" means written instructions that specify the strength, route, timing, titration, monitoring, contraindication checks, and discontinuation criteria for the packaged active agents.

"MODELED" means a statement derived from computational, repository, mechanistic, public-source, or pharmacologic rationale without completed combination testing.

"PROPHETIC" means a prospective example, study design, manufacturing protocol, or endpoint framework that has not been carried out for the disclosed fixed-dose product.

"DATA NEEDED" means evidence that is not presently represented by this package and must be generated or verified before final filing reliance, clinical use, or non-provisional claim expansion.

### 2. Active agents

Mianserin is the first active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a tetracyclic 5-HT2A/5-HT2C antagonist and α2-autoreceptor antagonist, with reference route oral, reference dosage form immediate-release tablet at bedtime, dose context 30 to 90 mg/day, metabolism or clearance CYP2D6 / CYP3A4 / CYP1A2, and safety liability agranulocytosis (rare); sedation; weight gain.

Testosterone propionate is the second active agent. Repository metadata identifies this active as a androgen receptor agonist, with reference route intramuscular, reference dosage form depot oily injection (every 2–3 days), dose context 25 to 100 mg/day, metabolism or clearance 5α-reductase; aromatase, and safety liability polycythemia; cardiovascular risk.

A pharmaceutically acceptable salt, hydrate, solvate, polymorph, isotopically labeled form, prodrug, or co-crystal is included only for embodiments with written-description, manufacturing, analytical, and clinical-pharmacology support.

### 3. Fixed-dose product strategy

The fixed-dose product strategy is fixed-dose combination product package with oral mianserin unit and intramuscular injection testosterone propionate unit.

MODELED: the active routes differ (mianserin: oral; testosterone propionate: intramuscular injection), so the fixed-dose product is drafted as a fixed labeled regimen with route-specific dosage units in one package rather than as an implausible co-formulated unit.

For same-route embodiments, a single physical fixed-dose dosage form may be used when compatibility, stability, release, content-uniformity, and bioavailability support are available. For mixed-route embodiments, the product is a package with route-specific dosage units and a fixed labeled regimen. For development-asset embodiments, clinical and CMC support are DATA NEEDED before any clinical-use assertion.

### 4. Dose ratios and strengths

MODELED: fixed-dose strengths are derived from repository individual-active dose contexts and are not completed combination dose-ranging results.

Nominal ratio range: 30 mg to 90 mg mianserin per 25 mg to 100 mg testosterone propionate.

| Strength level | Nominal daily fixed strength |
|---|---|
| low | 30 mg mianserin / 25 mg testosterone propionate |
| standard | 60 mg mianserin / 62.5 mg testosterone propionate |
| high | 90 mg mianserin / 100 mg testosterone propionate |

The disclosed strengths may be adjusted for renal function, hepatic function, age, body weight, sex, pharmacogenomic status, interacting medications, route-specific bioavailability, tolerability, and regulatory label constraints.

### 8. Safety, interactions, and monitoring

Administration includes baseline medication review, contraindication screening, adverse-event monitoring, endpoint tracking, and route-specific counseling.

* mianserin: agranulocytosis (rare); sedation; weight gain
* testosterone propionate: polycythemia; cardiovascular risk
* mianserin metabolism/clearance: CYP2D6 / CYP3A4 / CYP1A2
* testosterone propionate metabolism/clearance: 5α-reductase; aromatase
* route-specific administration errors are mitigated by unit-of-use packaging and fixed labeled timing instructions

Exclusion criteria include high-acuity psychiatric or medical states requiring immediate intervention, contraindications to either active agent, unsafe interacting medications, pregnancy or lactation concerns where relevant, inability to follow route-specific instructions, and inability to complete the monitoring plan.
