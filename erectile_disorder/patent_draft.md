## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Erectile Disorder by co-administering
(a) a tetracyclic 5-HT2A / 5-HT2C antagonist with alpha2-autoreceptor
antagonist activity and (b) an androgen receptor agonist, in
particular the combination of mianserin and testosterone propionate.
The invention is directed to adult male subjects with clinically
meaningful erectile dysfunction causing distress or functional sexual
impairment and is not intended for nonmedical sexual enhancement,
athletic or anabolic use, or unsupervised androgen administration.

## Background of the invention

Erectile Disorder is characterized by persistent difficulty attaining
or maintaining an erection sufficient for satisfactory sexual
activity, or by marked reduction in erectile rigidity or reliability,
causing clinically meaningful distress, relationship strain, or
functional impairment. The disorder may be lifelong or acquired,
generalized or situational, and may arise from vasculogenic,
endocrine, medication-related, psychogenic, neurogenic, or mixed
etiologies.

The clinical burden is substantial. Affected subjects may experience
reduced sexual confidence, avoidance of intimacy, partnership stress,
reduced self-esteem, and depressive or anxiety symptoms secondary to
repeated erectile failure. Even when desire is partly preserved,
sexual-attempt failure may progressively reinforce anticipatory
inhibition and sympathetic overcontrol.

Current standard care focuses on cardiovascular and endocrine
assessment, management of contributing medications or disease states,
and use of phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5) inhibitors. However, a
meaningful subset of patients has incomplete response to PDE5 therapy
or presents with a mixed phenotype involving low androgenic support,
serotonergic inhibition of sexual function, antidepressant-associated
sexual dysfunction, or attenuated central arousal readiness. There
remains an unmet need for a rational combination that addresses both
the androgenic substrate of erection and a central inhibitory tone
that can suppress erectile initiation.

The mechanistic gap addressed by the present invention has two layers.
First, testosterone signaling supports libido, nitric-oxide-linked
erectile responsiveness, penile tissue trophism, and general male
sexual function. Testosterone propionate is a short-acting androgen
receptor agonist that provides a controllable androgen-replacement or
androgen-support axis. Second, mianserin is a tetracyclic agent with
5-HT2A / 5-HT2C antagonism and alpha2-autoreceptor antagonism.
Excess serotonergic inhibitory tone can blunt sexual arousal and
erectile confidence, especially in subjects with medication-related or
anxious inhibitory phenotypes. The inventors recognized that mianserin
can reduce a central serotonergic brake on sexual arousal while
testosterone propionate restores the endocrine substrate necessary for
reliable erectile response.

The combination is therefore expected to be particularly useful in
adult men with erectile dysfunction associated with low or low-normal
androgen tone, antidepressant-associated erectile impairment,
attenuated nocturnal / morning erectile function, or incomplete
response to PDE5 inhibitor therapy alone. The invention does not
replace evaluation of vascular disease, diabetes, endocrine causes,
medication causes, or psychosexual contributors, but instead provides
a mechanism-based pharmacologic strategy for selected medically
appropriate subjects.

Prior-art searching identified multiple erectile-dysfunction treatment
references, testosterone-formulation references, and multi-agent
sexual-dysfunction references. Those disclosures do not teach the
specific combination of mianserin and testosterone propionate for
Erectile Disorder, nor the specific serotonergic-disinhibition plus
androgen-restoration framework disclosed herein.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) mianserin or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof and (b) testosterone
propionate or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt, ester formulation,
or prodrug thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically
acceptable carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Erectile Disorder in an adult male subject in need thereof,
comprising co-administering a therapeutically effective amount of
mianserin and a therapeutically effective amount of testosterone
propionate. In preferred embodiments, mianserin is administered
orally at bedtime and testosterone propionate is administered by
intramuscular depot injection every 2 to 3 days or on another
clinically appropriate replacement schedule.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) mianserin in a first dosage form, (b) testosterone propionate in a
second dosage form, and (c) written instructions for treatment of
Erectile Disorder under medical supervision, including instructions
for hematologic, endocrine, and prostate-safety monitoring where
appropriate.

The invention contemplates mianserin in a daily dose of about
30 mg to about 90 mg, preferably about 30 mg to about 60 mg,
administered orally at bedtime. The invention contemplates
testosterone propionate in a dose of about 25 mg to about 100 mg per
administration, typically given intramuscularly every 2 to 3 days or
on an equivalent clinically supervised regimen.

The technical effect of the combination is expected to be greater than
additive improvement in one or more endpoints selected from the group
consisting of erectile-function score, intercourse success rate,
morning-erection frequency, subjective erectile confidence, sexual-
distress burden, and Clinical Global Impression-Improvement (CGI-I),
compared with either monotherapy at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Erectile Disorder"* means a persistent disorder of erection
attainment, maintenance, rigidity, or reliability sufficient to
interfere with satisfactory sexual activity and causing clinically
meaningful distress or impairment in an adult male subject.

*"Mianserin"* means the tetracyclic antidepressant and
pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs,
prodrugs, and metabolically equivalent analogues that retain
clinically relevant 5-HT2A / 5-HT2C antagonism and alpha2 antagonism.

*"Testosterone propionate"* means testosterone propionate and related
pharmaceutically acceptable formulations, salts, solvates, prodrugs,
and equivalent short-acting androgen-support preparations that retain
clinically relevant androgen receptor agonist activity.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount that produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in one or more erectile-dysfunction
endpoints when used according to a regimen disclosed herein.

### 2. Active agents

#### 2.1 Mianserin

Mianserin is a tetracyclic 5-HT2A / 5-HT2C antagonist and
alpha2-autoreceptor antagonist. In the present invention, its
principal contribution is reduction of serotonergic inhibitory tone
and facilitation of central sexual-arousal readiness.

#### 2.2 Testosterone propionate

Testosterone propionate is a short-acting androgen receptor agonist.
In the present invention, its principal contribution is restoration or
support of androgenic sexual-function substrate relevant to libido,
erectile physiology, and erectile confidence.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise mianserin and
testosterone propionate together with one or more pharmaceutically
acceptable carriers or excipients. The actives may be provided as a
co-packaged kit containing an oral mianserin dosage form and an
injectable testosterone-propionate vial or prefilled syringe.

Suitable excipients for mianserin include common oral-tablet
excipients such as diluents, binders, disintegrants, lubricants, and
coating agents. Suitable excipients for testosterone propionate
include pharmaceutically acceptable oily depot vehicles and stabilizer
systems appropriate for intramuscular injection.

### 4. Dosing regimens

For adult male subjects with Erectile Disorder, a preferred regimen
comprises:

* Mianserin 30 mg to 90 mg orally once daily, preferably at bedtime.
* Testosterone propionate 25 mg to 100 mg intramuscularly every 2 to
  3 days or on an equivalent clinically supervised androgen-support
  schedule.

In certain embodiments mianserin is initiated first and titrated to a
tolerated bedtime dose before androgen therapy is added. In other
embodiments testosterone propionate is initiated first in subjects
with documented hypogonadism or low-normal testosterone and mianserin
is added in subjects with residual central inhibitory symptoms or
antidepressant-associated sexual dysfunction.

In preferred embodiments the subject's PDE5 inhibitor regimen, if any,
is stable so that incremental benefit of the present combination can
be assessed.

### 5. Subject populations

Preferred subjects are adult men with clinician-confirmed Erectile
Disorder, especially those with incomplete response to PDE5
inhibitors, low or low-normal testosterone, antidepressant-associated
erectile dysfunction, reduced morning erections, or mixed desire-
arousal impairment.

Exclusion criteria for preferred embodiments include known or
suspected prostate carcinoma requiring alternate management, severe
uncontrolled cardiovascular disease making sexual activity unsafe,
polycythemia, untreated severe sleep apnea, hypersensitivity to either
active, and use sought primarily for athletic enhancement or
nonmedical sexual enhancement.

### 6. Mechanistic rationale

Erectile Disorder often reflects both a peripheral physiologic
component and a central inhibitory component. Restoring androgenic
support alone may be insufficient if the subject remains under a
strong serotonergic or anxiously inhibitory sexual brake. Likewise,
reducing inhibitory tone may be insufficient if the endocrine and
androgenic substrate for erection remains inadequate.

Mianserin addresses the central inhibitory layer by antagonizing
5-HT2A / 5-HT2C-mediated sexual-function suppression and reducing a
serotonergic brake on arousal. Testosterone propionate addresses the
androgenic-support layer by improving the hormonal substrate for
sexual function. The combination is therefore expected to produce
larger improvements in erectile reliability and sexual confidence than
either agent alone at any individually achievable dose.

### 7. Administration and monitoring

Monitoring may include erectile-function questionnaires such as IIEF-
EF, intercourse success diary, morning-erection frequency, sexual-
distress assessment, hematocrit, testosterone levels, liver function
if clinically indicated, and prostate-safety assessment according to
standard endocrine or sexual-medicine practice.

The regimen is not intended to replace vascular risk evaluation,
diabetes management, medication review, psychosexual counseling,
cardiovascular clearance where appropriate, or endocrine workup that
remain essential to comprehensive Erectile Disorder care.

### 8. Prophetic clinical study

A prophetic Phase 2 study enrolls adult men with persistent Erectile
Disorder, baseline impairment on IIEF-EF, and either inadequate PDE5
response or a low-androgen / serotonergic-inhibition phenotype.
Subjects are randomized to: (a) mianserin plus testosterone
propionate; (b) testosterone propionate plus placebo; or (c) placebo
plus standard medical care, for 12 weeks.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in erectile-function
score. Key secondary endpoints include intercourse success rate,
morning-erection frequency, subjective erectile confidence,
sexual-distress burden, and CGI-I. The combination arm is expected to
show the largest improvement in erectile reliability and confidence
with acceptable tolerability under supervision. FIGS. 3-5 are
prophetic illustrations of those expected outcomes.

### 9. Prior-art distinction

The closest prior-art references identified in the prior-art
landscape include:

* Testosterone-formulation and testosterone-deficiency references -
  disclose androgen replacement, but do not teach mianserin
  co-administration for Erectile Disorder.
* Erectile-dysfunction treatment references - disclose PDE5-centered
  or other different approaches, not the specific serotonergic-
  disinhibition plus androgen-support combination disclosed herein.
* Multi-agent sexual-dysfunction references - do not teach the
  specific actives and dosing framework of mianserin plus testosterone
  propionate.

The present invention is distinct in the specific receptor classes
combined, the specific actives chosen, and the framework of treating a
mixed serotonergic-inhibitory plus androgen-deficient erectile
phenotype.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of Erectile
Disorder comprising co-administration of a therapeutically effective
amount of the tetracyclic 5-HT2A / 5-HT2C antagonist mianserin and a
therapeutically effective amount of the androgen receptor agonist
testosterone propionate are disclosed. The two agents act on non-
overlapping serotonergic-inhibitory and androgen-support pathways
relevant to erectile function and are expected to produce greater-
than-additive improvement in erectile reliability, intercourse
success, and sexual confidence compared with either agent alone at any
individually achievable dose under medical supervision.
