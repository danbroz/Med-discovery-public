# Fixed-Dose Evidence Appendix

Application: `026_opioid_withdrawal`
Disorder: Opioid Withdrawal
Active pair: sufentanil + oxymetazoline
Route feasibility classification: **multi_unit_fixed_regimen**

## Evidence Standard

This appendix uses public-source search links, repository medication metadata, local patents-rag results, and MODELED or PROPHETIC rationale. It does not report completed combination testing, actual fixed-dose stability, actual dissolution, actual PK interaction data, or completed component-contribution results.

## Dose Ratio and Preferred Strengths

Nominal ratio range: 0.015 mg to 0.12 mg sufentanil per 0.5 mg to 2 mg oxymetazoline.

| Level | Nominal daily fixed strength | Label |
|---|---|---|
| low | 0.015 mg sufentanil / 0.5 mg oxymetazoline | MODELED |
| standard | 0.0675 mg sufentanil / 1.25 mg oxymetazoline | MODELED |
| high | 0.12 mg sufentanil / 2 mg oxymetazoline | MODELED |

## Route and Dosage Form

MODELED: the active routes differ (sufentanil: sublingual; oxymetazoline: intranasal), so the fixed-dose product is drafted as a fixed labeled regimen with route-specific dosage units in one package rather than as an implausible co-formulated unit.

Product strategy: fixed-dose combination product package with sublingual sufentanil unit and intranasal oxymetazoline unit.

## Compatibility, Stability, and Release

MODELED: route-specific units reduce direct chemical contact between actives. Stability support must still be generated for the assembled kit, labeling, container closure, and each constituent product under proposed storage conditions.

MODELED route-specific release: sufentanil follows the sublingual performance standard and oxymetazoline follows the intranasal performance standard; no co-dissolution or single-matrix release is asserted.

Stability status: DATA NEEDED. Required studies include forced degradation, accelerated and long-term stability, assay and impurity methods, container-closure compatibility, and in-use testing where applicable.

## Manufacturability

- MODELED: manufacture or source a fixed-strength sublingual dosage unit for sufentanil
- MODELED: manufacture or source a fixed-strength intranasal dosage unit for oxymetazoline
- MODELED: assemble route-specific units in a single blister, carton, tray, or labeled kit
- MODELED: include fixed regimen instructions, timing controls, contraindication screening, and discontinuation criteria
- MODELED: qualify container-closure and shipping conditions for each constituent unit

## Component Contribution

sufentanil: MODELED: sufentanil supplies the selective μ-opioid receptor agonist mechanism axis for the Opioid Withdrawal regimen.

oxymetazoline: MODELED: oxymetazoline supplies the α1A/α2A adrenergic agonist mechanism axis for the Opioid Withdrawal regimen.

PROPHETIC: evaluate fixed-dose sufentanil + oxymetazoline against sufentanil alone, oxymetazoline alone, placebo or stable standard care, and a dose-ratio arm in subjects with confirmed Opioid Withdrawal; predefine primary endpoint, safety hierarchy, PK sampling, adherence assessment, and criteria for component contribution.

## Safety and Interaction Controls

- sufentanil: respiratory depression; abuse liability (Schedule II)
- oxymetazoline: rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa)
- sufentanil metabolism/clearance: CYP3A4
- oxymetazoline metabolism/clearance: minimal hepatic; predominantly excreted unchanged
- sufentanil-containing embodiments require medically supervised administration, respiratory-depression controls, abuse-liability controls, and rescue-medication availability
- route-specific administration errors are mitigated by unit-of-use packaging and fixed labeled timing instructions
