## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Schizophrenia by co-administering
(a) a serotonin 5-HT2A receptor inverse agonist / antagonist and
(b) a dopamine D2 / 5-HT2A antagonist atypical antipsychotic, in
particular the combination of pimavanserin and perospirone. The
invention is directed to adult subjects with schizophrenia and
addresses hallucinations, delusions, thought disorganization, salience
misattribution, and functional impairment while seeking to reduce the
need for indiscriminate escalation of D2 burden. The invention does
not assert cure, disease modification, or replacement of emergency
management, clozapine where indicated, or other standard antipsychotic
care pathways.

## Background of the invention

Schizophrenia is a chronic psychotic disorder characterized by
hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thought, negative symptoms,
cognitive inefficiency, social and occupational dysfunction, and high
relapse burden. The clinical and social burden is substantial,
including recurrent hospitalization, caregiver strain, reduced life
expectancy, functional disability, and impaired community
participation.

Current antipsychotic therapy is dominated by D2 receptor antagonism
or partial agonism. Although such strategies can reduce positive
symptoms, they are often limited by extrapyramidal symptoms,
hyperprolactinemia, sedation, metabolic burden, incomplete efficacy,
and persistent residual hallucinations or delusional salience.
Patients with partial response frequently undergo dose escalation or
polypharmacy with uncertain mechanistic rationale. There remains a
need for a combination strategy that modulates psychotic salience more
specifically while maintaining a proven D2 antipsychotic anchor.

The mechanistic gap addressed by the present invention has two layers.
First, pimavanserin is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist /
antagonist with weak 5-HT2C activity and essentially no D2 affinity.
The inventors recognized that 5-HT2A-mediated perceptual distortion
and salience amplification are directly relevant to psychotic
experience and that attenuation of this axis may reduce hallucination-
like and delusion-reinforcing processing without adding direct D2
motor burden. Second, perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic with D2
and 5-HT2A antagonism plus 5-HT1A partial agonist activity. In the
present invention, perospirone provides the dopaminergic-stabilization
anchor that controls core positive-symptom salience and disorganized
behavior.

The inventors recognized that these mechanisms are complementary and
non-redundant. Pimavanserin provides a non-D2 5-HT2A salience-
modulating axis, while perospirone provides a D2 antipsychotic axis.
Together, the two are expected to reduce psychotic symptom burden more
effectively than either monotherapy at any individually achievable
dose, while allowing lower or more tolerable D2 exposure than would
otherwise be required in selected patients.

Prior-art searching identified pimavanserin formulation references,
pimavanserin combination references, schizophrenia treatment
references, and general antipsychotic-combination references. Those
references do not teach the specific combination of pimavanserin and
perospirone for Schizophrenia, nor the explicit framework of pairing
5-HT2A inverse agonism with a D2 antipsychotic anchor to reduce
salience pathology and positive symptoms.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) pimavanserin or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof and (b) perospirone or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Schizophrenia in a human subject in need thereof, comprising
co-administering a therapeutically effective amount of pimavanserin
and a therapeutically effective amount of perospirone. Co-
administration may be simultaneous or sequential, with each agent
administered within a window of about 24 hours of the other.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) pimavanserin in a first dosage form, (b) perospirone in a second
dosage form, and (c) written instructions for use in adult subjects
with schizophrenia, including guidance regarding QT monitoring where
appropriate, extrapyramidal symptom assessment, prolactin-related
tolerability, and escalation to higher-acuity or clozapine pathways
when clinically required.

The invention contemplates pimavanserin in a daily dose of about
17 mg to about 34 mg administered orally once daily, with a preferred
adult dose of 34 mg/day. The invention contemplates perospirone in a
daily dose of about 4 mg to about 48 mg administered orally in one or
two divided doses, with a preferred dose of about 8 mg to about
24 mg/day.

The technical effect of the combination is expected to be greater than
additive improvement in one or more endpoints selected from the group
consisting of positive-symptom burden, hallucination burden, delusion
severity, disorganization score, negative-symptom-linked functional
engagement, and Clinical Global Impression-Improvement (CGI-I),
compared with either monotherapy at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have the
meanings set forth below.

*"Schizophrenia"* means schizophrenia as defined by DSM-5-TR, ICD-11,
or an equivalent diagnostic framework, characterized by psychotic
symptoms such as hallucinations, delusions, disorganization, or
related positive symptoms together with clinically meaningful
functional impairment.

*"Pimavanserin"* means the selective serotonin 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist and its pharmaceutically acceptable salts,
solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, labeled forms, and
pharmacologically equivalent 5-HT2A inverse agonists.

*"Perospirone"* means the atypical antipsychotic marketed in Japan for
schizophrenia and includes the free base, pharmaceutically acceptable
salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs, prodrugs, labeled forms, and
metabolically equivalent analogues that retain clinically relevant D2
and 5-HT2A antagonism.

*"Psychotic-symptom burden"* means a clinically meaningful burden of
hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thought, formal thought
disorder, psychotic salience, or related positive symptoms quantified
by structured clinician assessment or validated psychosis instruments.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount that produces a
clinically meaningful improvement in one or more schizophrenia
endpoints when used according to a regimen disclosed herein.

### 2. Active agents

#### 2.1 Pimavanserin

Pimavanserin is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist with
essentially no clinically meaningful D2 affinity. In the present
invention, its principal contribution is attenuation of salience
amplification, perceptual distortion, and hallucinatory or delusion-
reinforcing cortical processing.

#### 2.2 Perospirone

Perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic with D2 and 5-HT2A
antagonism plus 5-HT1A partial agonist activity. In the present
invention, its principal contribution is attenuation of dopaminergic
salience dyscontrol, positive symptoms, and disorganized behavior.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise pimavanserin and
perospirone together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients. The actives may be provided as separate oral
tablets or capsules, as a co-packaged kit, or as a synchronized blister-
card system aligned to once-daily pimavanserin use and once- or twice-
daily perospirone dosing.

Suitable excipients include common oral-tablet or capsule diluents,
binders, disintegrants, lubricants, coating systems, and release-
control materials. In preferred embodiments pimavanserin is maintained
once daily and perospirone is titrated according to positive-symptom
burden and tolerability.

### 4. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with Schizophrenia, a preferred regimen comprises:

* Pimavanserin 17 mg to 34 mg orally once daily.
* Perospirone 4 mg to 48 mg orally per day, preferably 8 mg to
  24 mg/day in one or two divided doses.

In certain embodiments perospirone is initiated or optimized first to
provide core positive-symptom containment and pimavanserin is added in
subjects with persistent hallucinations, residual delusional salience,
or D2-burden-limited tolerability. In other embodiments pimavanserin
is layered in from the outset where a lower total D2 exposure is
desired.

In preferred embodiments the subject's background psychosocial care,
case management, or stable additional pharmacotherapy is maintained so
that the incremental effect of the present combination can be
meaningfully assessed.

### 5. Subject populations

Preferred subjects are adults with clinician-confirmed schizophrenia,
especially subjects with persistent hallucinations, delusions,
disorganization, partial response to prior antipsychotics, or D2-
burden-limited tolerability. Certain embodiments focus on subjects
with residual psychotic salience despite standard antipsychotic care.

Exclusion criteria for preferred embodiments include acute violent or
severely disorganized psychosis requiring immediate emergency
containment, known intolerance to either active, severe uncontrolled
QT-risk context, severe drug interaction burden that precludes safe
use, and dementia-related psychosis outside the intended adult
schizophrenia population.

### 6. Mechanistic rationale

Schizophrenia often persists because D2-mediated salience dyscontrol
and 5-HT2A-linked perceptual or interpretive distortion coexist and
reinforce one another. Increasing D2 blockade alone may reduce some
positive symptoms but can also increase motor, endocrine, and
tolerability burden. A non-D2 salience-modulating mechanism may
therefore complement a D2 antipsychotic anchor.

Pimavanserin addresses the 5-HT2A-linked perceptual-salience layer.
Perospirone addresses the dopaminergic-salience and behavioral-
disorganization layer. The combination is therefore expected to
produce larger improvements in positive symptoms and disorganization
than either agent alone at any individually achievable dose.

### 7. Administration and monitoring

Monitoring may include PANSS or other psychosis scales, hallucination
and delusion burden, QT-risk monitoring where clinically appropriate,
extrapyramidal symptoms, prolactin-related adverse effects, metabolic
status, functional engagement, and CGI-I.

The regimen is not intended to replace emergency psychiatric care,
clozapine for established treatment-resistant schizophrenia when
indicated, case management, psychosocial rehabilitation, or other
standard antipsychotic-care pathways.

### 8. Prophetic clinical study

A prophetic Phase 2 study enrolls adults with schizophrenia, baseline
positive-symptom burden, and incomplete response or limited
tolerability on existing antipsychotic care. Subjects are randomized
to: (a) pimavanserin plus perospirone; (b) perospirone plus placebo; or
(c) placebo plus stable standard of care, for 12 weeks after titration.

The primary endpoint is change from baseline in psychotic-symptom
burden. Key secondary endpoints include hallucination burden, delusion
severity, disorganization score, functional engagement, and CGI-I. The
combination arm is expected to show the largest improvement in
positive symptoms and functional participation with acceptable
tolerability. FIGS. 3-5 are prophetic illustrations of those expected
outcomes.

### 9. Prior-art distinction

The closest prior-art references identified in the prior-art
landscape include:

* Pimavanserin formulation and pimavanserin-combination references -
  disclose pimavanserin individually or with different partner drugs,
  not the present pimavanserin + perospirone pairing.
* Schizophrenia treatment references - disclose other antipsychotic
  strategies without teaching the specific 5-HT2A inverse agonist plus
  D2 antipsychotic framework disclosed herein.
* General psychiatric combination references - do not teach the
  specific actives and salience-layering strategy of the present
  invention.

The present invention is distinct in the specific receptor classes
combined, the specific actives chosen, and the framework of pairing a
non-D2 5-HT2A inverse agonist with a D2 antipsychotic anchor in
Schizophrenia.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Schizophrenia comprising co-administration of a therapeutically
effective amount of the serotonin 5-HT2A receptor inverse agonist /
antagonist pimavanserin and a therapeutically effective amount of the
atypical antipsychotic perospirone are disclosed. The two agents act
on non-overlapping 5-HT2A salience-modulation and D2 antipsychotic
pathways relevant to schizophrenia and are expected to produce
greater-than-additive improvement in positive symptoms, disorganization,
and functional engagement compared with either agent alone at any
individually achievable dose.
