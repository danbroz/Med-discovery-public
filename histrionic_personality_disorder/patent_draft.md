## Field of the invention

The present invention relates to pharmaceutical compositions and to
methods of treating Histrionic Personality Disorder (HPD) by
co-administering (a) a dopamine D2 receptor antagonist and (b) a
serotonin 5-HT2A receptor inverse agonist / antagonist, in
particular the combination of perospirone and pimavanserin. The
invention is intended for adult subjects with HPD who are
concurrently receiving structured psychotherapy or supportive
behavioral treatment, and addresses excessive emotionality,
attention-seeking, affective lability, and maladaptive social-
salience amplification.

## Background of the invention

Histrionic Personality Disorder is a personality disorder defined in
DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 by a pervasive pattern of excessive emotionality
and attention-seeking behavior, beginning by early adulthood and
present across contexts. Characteristic features include discomfort
in situations in which the individual is not the center of
attention, inappropriate sexually seductive or provocative
interaction, rapidly shifting and shallow expression of emotions,
use of physical appearance to draw attention, impressionistic or
lacking-in-detail speech, self-dramatization, theatricality, and
suggestibility. The clinical problem is not simply sociability but
the instability, exaggeration, and externally anchored salience
structure of emotional life.

The prevalence of HPD in the general population is low, but the
clinical burden is meaningful because the disorder is associated
with unstable relationships, occupational difficulty, high
interpersonal conflict, low treatment retention, and frequent
comorbidity with anxiety, depression, trauma-spectrum symptoms, and
substance use. The disorder may produce recurrent crisis
presentations driven by shame, rejection, or rapid shifts in
perceived relational value.

The current standard of care is non-pharmacological in the first
instance and consists of psychotherapy, supportive treatment, and
management of comorbid disorders. No pharmaceutical product is
approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
for the treatment of HPD as a primary indication. Where medication
is used, clinicians generally borrow from anxiety, mood, or
impulsivity practice, but these approaches are inconsistently
effective and may not directly address the exaggerated salience
assigned to attention, approval, and emotionally charged social
feedback.

The mechanistic gap that the present invention addresses has two
components. First, the interpersonal dysregulation of HPD may be
maintained by dopaminergic reinforcement of attention-seeking,
novelty-seeking, and emotionally charged behavioral output.
Perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic approved in Japan for
schizophrenia that functions as a D2 receptor antagonist with
additional serotonergic properties. In the present invention, the
D2-antagonist axis addresses the *reinforcement of dramatic,
attention-seeking, and poorly regulated behavioral output*
substrate of HPD.

Second, HPD is characterized by maladaptive over-weighting of
social attention, approval, rejection, and emotionally evocative
cues. Pimavanserin (Nuplazid®) is a selective 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist approved by the FDA for hallucinations and
delusions associated with Parkinson disease psychosis. Pimavanserin
has no clinically meaningful D2 antagonism and therefore can modify
5-HT2A-mediated cortical salience physiology without directly
increasing dopaminergic motor burden. The selective 5-HT2A axis
therefore addresses the *maladaptive attention salience, emotional
amplification, and theatrical cue weighting* substrate of HPD.

No issued patent or published application known to the present
inventors discloses the use of a D2 antagonist in combination with
a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist for HPD
specifically.

The present invention recognizes that attenuation of dopaminergic
reinforcement of dramatic / attention-seeking output (a perospirone
D2 axis) and attenuation of 5-HT2A-mediated social-salience
amplification (a pimavanserin axis) are complementary and
convergent substrates for treatment of HPD. The combination is
expected to produce greater symptomatic improvement in affective
lability, attention-seeking behavior, and interpersonal stability
than either agent alone at any individually achievable dose.

## Brief summary of the invention

In one aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition
comprising (a) perospirone or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt,
solvate, polymorph, or prodrug thereof, and (b) pimavanserin or a
pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, polymorph, or prodrug
thereof, together with one or more pharmaceutically acceptable
carriers or excipients.

In another aspect, the invention provides a method of treating
Histrionic Personality Disorder in an adult subject in need
thereof, comprising co-administering to the subject a
therapeutically effective amount of perospirone and a
therapeutically effective amount of pimavanserin. Co-administration
may be simultaneous or sequential, with each agent administered
within a window of about 24 hours of the other.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a kit comprising
(a) perospirone or a salt thereof in a first unitary dosage form,
(b) pimavanserin or a salt thereof in a second unitary dosage form,
and (c) written instructions for the treatment of HPD according to
a dosing regimen disclosed herein in conjunction with psychotherapy
or supportive treatment.

The invention contemplates perospirone in a daily dose of about
4 mg to about 48 mg administered orally in one or two divided
doses, with a preferred adult dose of 8 mg to 24 mg/day. The
invention contemplates pimavanserin in a daily dose of about 17 mg
to about 34 mg administered orally once daily, with a preferred
adult dose of 34 mg/day.

The technical effect of the combination is a reduction, in adult
subjects with HPD, in excessive emotional lability, attention-
seeking intensity, crisis-prone interpersonal dyscontrol, and
partner / caregiver burden, that is greater than the sum of the
individual effects of perospirone and pimavanserin administered as
monotherapies at any individually achievable dose.

## Detailed description of the invention

### 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this specification, the following terms have
the meanings set forth below.

*"Perospirone"* means the atypical antipsychotic marketed in Japan
for schizophrenia that acts as an antagonist at dopamine D2
receptors, an inverse agonist / antagonist at 5-HT2A receptors, and
a partial agonist at 5-HT1A receptors. The term includes
perospirone free base, salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs,
prodrugs, isotopically labeled forms, and metabolically equivalent
analogues.

*"Pimavanserin"* means the selective serotonin 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist marketed in the United States as Nuplazid®.
The term includes pimavanserin free base, tartrate or other
pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates, hydrates, polymorphs,
prodrugs, isotopically labeled forms, and pharmacologically
equivalent 5-HT2A inverse agonists.

*"Histrionic Personality Disorder"* or *"HPD"* means the personality
disorder defined in DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 by a pervasive pattern of
excessive emotionality and attention-seeking behavior beginning by
early adulthood and present across contexts.

*"Affective-lability / attention-seeking score"* means a clinically
meaningful burden of rapidly shifting emotional expression,
theatricality, attention-seeking behavior, or impressionistic /
suggestible interpersonal responding quantified by structured
clinician assessment or validated personality / symptom
instruments.

*"Therapeutically effective amount"* means an amount of an active
agent which, when administered to an adult subject for the
treatment of HPD according to a dosing regimen disclosed herein,
produces a clinically meaningful improvement in at least one of:
affective-lability / attention-seeking score, theatrical behavior
score, interpersonal-function score, partner / caregiver burden, or
investigator-rated CGI-I.

*"Co-administer"* means administer two active agents to a subject
so that the pharmacological effect of one agent is exerted on the
subject during the period in which the pharmacological effect of
the other agent is also exerted, regardless of whether the two
agents are administered in the same dosage form, in separate
dosage forms, at the same time, or at different times within a
window of about 24 hours.

### 2. The active agents

#### 2.1 Perospirone

Perospirone is an atypical antipsychotic developed and approved in
Japan for schizophrenia. It acts as a D2 antagonist, 5-HT2A
inverse agonist / antagonist, and 5-HT1A partial agonist. In the
present invention, the principal mechanistic contribution of
perospirone is D2 antagonism directed toward reduction of
attention-seeking reinforcement and poorly regulated dramatic
behavioral output. Perospirone is generally used in adult
schizophrenia at oral doses in the range of about 8 mg to
48 mg/day.

#### 2.2 Pimavanserin

Pimavanserin is a selective 5-HT2A inverse agonist / antagonist
approved by the FDA for hallucinations and delusions associated
with Parkinson disease psychosis. It has no clinically meaningful
affinity for dopamine D2 receptors. In the present invention, the
principal mechanistic contribution of pimavanserin is modification
of maladaptive social-attention salience and emotional
amplification without direct D2 burden. The labeled adult dose is
34 mg orally once daily, with no dose titration required.

### 3. Compositions

The compositions of the present invention comprise perospirone and
pimavanserin, together with at least one pharmaceutically
acceptable excipient. The compositions may take the form of a
co-packaged kit of two separate dosage forms, such as a perospirone
tablet and a pimavanserin capsule or tablet.

Excipients useful for the perospirone composition include diluents
(microcrystalline cellulose, lactose monohydrate), binders
(povidone, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose), disintegrants
(crospovidone), lubricants (magnesium stearate), and coating
polymers. Excipients useful for the pimavanserin composition
include capsule fill excipients, diluents, disintegrants, and
stabilizers suitable for the marketed pimavanserin formulation
profile.

### 4. Dosage forms

Perospirone is preferably formulated as an immediate-release oral
tablet. Pimavanserin is preferably formulated as an oral capsule or
tablet suitable for once-daily dosing.

A kit form of the invention comprises (a) a 30-day supply of
perospirone tablets, (b) a 30-day supply of pimavanserin capsules
or tablets, and (c) written instructions aligned to psychotherapy,
emotion-regulation goals, and adherence monitoring.

### 5. Dosing regimens

For adult subjects with HPD, the preferred dosing regimen is:

* Perospirone 4 mg orally twice daily for 7 days, then 8 mg twice
  daily thereafter, with titration up to 12 mg twice daily if
  clinically needed.
* Pimavanserin 34 mg orally once daily.

Broad genus ranges include perospirone 4–48 mg/day and
pimavanserin 17–34 mg/day. The preferred schedule uses a morning
and evening perospirone regimen and a once-daily pimavanserin
regimen at a consistent clock time.

Hepatic and renal adjustment: severe hepatic or renal impairment is
exclusionary until dedicated dose-finding data are generated in
this indication.

Medication–medication interactions: avoid co-administration with
strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers without review of pimavanserin
exposure. Corrected QT interval monitoring should be considered
when other QT-prolonging medications are used.

### 6. Subject populations

The methods of the present invention are directed to adult subjects
with HPD. Inclusion criteria for the preferred treatment population
include: age ≥ 18 years; clinician-confirmed HPD; clinically
meaningful emotional lability, attention-seeking behavior, or
theatrical interpersonal dyscontrol at baseline; and capacity to
participate in psychotherapy or supportive behavioral treatment.
Exclusion criteria include schizophrenia, active psychotic
disorder, active mania, uncontrolled substance use disorder,
severe hepatic or renal impairment, and known hypersensitivity to
either active.

### 7. Mechanistic rationale

HPD is characterized by two interacting layers: maladaptive
attention salience and reinforcement of dramatic, affectively
labile, and approval-seeking behavior. These layers can result in
shallow but intense affect displays, unstable alliance formation,
and repeated crisis behavior.

Perospirone addresses the reinforcement / behavioral-output layer
through D2 antagonism. Pimavanserin addresses the maladaptive
social-attention and emotional-amplification layer through 5-HT2A
inverse agonism without direct D2 burden. The two axes are
non-overlapping at the receptor level and converge on the
affective-lability / attention-seeking phenotype.

Co-administration is therefore expected to produce greater-than-
additive reduction in dramatic emotional dyscontrol and
interpersonal instability than either monotherapy alone. The
master receptor-efficacy summary
(`all_disorders_taxonomy_aware_master_summary.csv`), is 31,
indicating mechanistic compatibility above the reporting threshold
for the indication.

### 8. Pre-clinical support

In-vitro assays useful for confirming the mechanistic basis of the
invention include: D2 receptor binding and functional antagonism
assays for perospirone; 5-HT2A binding and inverse-agonism assays
for pimavanserin; and combined social-salience network assays where
available.

In-vivo paradigms appropriate for probing the relevant substrates
include social-salience, novelty-seeking, affective reactivity, and
attention-capture paradigms. No animal model is asserted to
reproduce HPD itself; rather, the preclinical program supports
convergent mechanisms of exaggerated social salience and dramatic
behavioral reinforcement.

### 9. Clinical study designs

#### 9.1 Phase 1 — pharmacokinetics and medication–medication interaction

A single-center, open-label, three-period, fixed-sequence study in
24 healthy adult volunteers (age 18–55 years). Period 1:
perospirone titrated to 16 mg/day for 7 days; Period 2:
pimavanserin 34 mg/day for 7 days; Period 3: the combination for
7 days. Full PK sampling, ECG monitoring, and tolerability
monitoring are obtained in each period. The principal purpose is to
establish tolerability and absence of clinically meaningful PK
interaction.

#### 9.2 Phase 2 — randomized, double-blind, three-arm study

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study of
the combination in adult HPD (FIG. 4). Three arms, 1:1:1
randomization, target N = 120 (40 per arm).

* Arm A (combination): perospirone + pimavanserin.
* Arm B (active monotherapy): pimavanserin + placebo for
  perospirone.
* Arm C (placebo): placebo for both, with structured psychotherapy.

All subjects receive standardized psychotherapy or supportive
behavioral treatment focused on affect regulation, alliance
formation, and attention-seeking behavior. The primary endpoint is
change from baseline in affective-lability / attention-seeking
score at week 12. Secondary endpoints include theatrical behavior
score, interpersonal-function score, responder rate, partner /
caregiver burden, and CGI-I.

## Abstract

A pharmaceutical composition and method for the treatment of
Histrionic Personality Disorder comprising co-administration of a
therapeutically effective amount of the D2 antagonist perospirone
and a therapeutically effective amount of the 5-HT2A inverse
agonist / antagonist pimavanserin are disclosed. The two agents act
on non-overlapping dramatic-behavior reinforcement and
social-attention salience circuits implicated in excessive
emotionality and attention-seeking behavior, and are expected to
produce greater-than-additive symptomatic improvement than either
agent alone at any individually achievable dose.
